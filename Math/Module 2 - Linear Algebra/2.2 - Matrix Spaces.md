## Matrix Spaces

> [!def] Rank
> The **rank** of a [[2.2 - Matrices#^5bf009]] $\mathbf{A}$ is equal to the [[2.2 - Subspaces#^dimension]] of the [[2.2 - Matrices#^column-space]] or the [[2.2 - Matrices#^661cf7]]. It's also equal to the number of [[2.3 - RREF and Systems of Equations#^pivot]] columns or rows.
>
> $$rank(\mathbf{A})\equiv dim(col(\mathbf{A}))\equiv dim(row(\mathbf{A}))$$

^rank

> [!thm] Rank-Nullity Theorem
> For any $m\times n$ matrix $\mathbf{A}$, the [[2.2 - Matrices#^rank]] plus the [[2.2 - Subspaces#^dimension]] of the [[#^null-space]] is equal to the number of columns $n$.
> $$rank(\mathbf{A})+dim(null(\mathbf{A}))=n$$

### Row Space

> [!def] Row Space
> The [[2.2 - Subspaces#^span]] of the *rows* $\{\mathbf{a}_{i} \}$ of an $m\times n$ [[2.2 - Matrices#^5bf009]] $\mathbf{A}$ is the **row space** $row(\mathbf{A})$.
> $$col\left(\begin{bmatrix}
\mathbf{a}_1 \\ \vdots \\\mathbf{a}_m
\end{bmatrix}\right)\equiv span(\{\mathbf{a}_{1},\dots,\mathbf{a}_{n}\})$$

^661cf7

> [!thm] Basis of the Row Space
> The [[2.3 - RREF and Systems of Equations#^pivot]] rows of $\mathbf{A}$ form a [[2.2 - Subspaces#^basis]] for the [[#^661cf7]] $row(\mathbf{A})$.

### Column Space

> [!def] Column Space
> The [[2.2 - Subspaces#^span]] of the *columns* $\{\mathbf{a}_{j} \}$ of an $m\times n$ [[2.2 - Matrices#^5bf009]] $\mathbf{A}$ is the **column space** $col(\mathbf{A})$.
>
> $$col([\mathbf{a}_{1},\dots,\mathbf{a}_{n}])\equiv span(\{\mathbf{a}_{1},\dots,\mathbf{a}_{n} \})$$
>

^column-space

> [!thm] Basis of the Column Space
> The [[2.3 - RREF and Systems of Equations#^pivot]] columns of $\mathbf{A}$ form a [[2.2 - Subspaces#^basis]] for the [[#^column-space]] $col(\mathbf{A})$.

^basis-of-the-column-space

### Null Space

> [!def] Null Space
> For an $m\times n$ [[2.2 - Matrices#^5bf009]] $\mathbf{A}$, the **null space** (or **kernel**) $ker(\mathbf{A})$ is the set of all solutions to $\mathbf{A}\,\mathbf{x}=0$.
>
> $$null(\mathbf{A})=\{\mathbf{x}:\mathbf{A}\,\mathbf{x}=\mathbf{0} \}$$
>

^null-space

> [!thm] Null Space is a Subspace
> The [[#^null-space]] is a [[2.2 - Subspaces#^subspace]].

^null-space-is-a-subspace

`\begin{proof}`@[[#^null-space-is-a-subspace]]
Let there be two vectors $(\mathbf{x}_{1}, \ \mathbf{x}_{2})\in ker(\mathbf{A})$. Therefore, $\mathbf{A}\,\mathbf{x}_{1}=0$, $\mathbf{A}\,\mathbf{x}_{2}=0$.

$$
\begin{align}
A(c_{1}\,\mathbf{x}_{1}+c_{2}\,\mathbf{x}_{2})=c_{1}\,\mathbf{A}\,\mathbf{x}_{1}+c_{2}\,\mathbf{A}\,\mathbf{x}_{2}=\mathbf{0}
\end{align}
$$

Therefore, $(c_{1}\,\mathbf{x}_{1}+c_{2}\,\mathbf{x}_{2})\in ker(\mathbf{A})$, so $ker(\mathbf{A})$ is a subspace.
`\end{proof}`

> [!thm] Basis of the Null Space
> Each [[2.3 - RREF and Systems of Equations#^basic-solution]] to $\mathbf{A}\,\mathbf{x}=0$ is a [[2.2 - Subspaces#^basis]] vector for the [[#^null-space]].
>

^basis-of-the-null-space
