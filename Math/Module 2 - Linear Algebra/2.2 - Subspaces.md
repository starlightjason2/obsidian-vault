# 2.2 - Subspaces

> [!def] Subspace
> A set $V\subset \mathbb{R}^{n}$ is a **subspace** of a [[2.1 - Vector Spaces#^87cfc7]] when the [[2.1 - Vectors#^93b383]] of any two vectors $(\mathbf{u},\mathbf{v})\in V$ is also in $V$.
>
> $$V \text{ is a subspace of } \mathbb{R}^{n}\iff (\mathbf{u},\mathbf{v})\in V \implies( c_{1}\,\mathbf{u}+c_{2}\,\mathbf{v})\in V$$
>

^420b21

> [!def] Real Space
> $\mathbb{R}^{n}$ is a [[#^420b21]] of [[3.6 - Complex Numbers#^8288b7]] formed by every real-valued [[2.1 - Vector Spaces#^f36929]] with [[#^09a800]] $n$.
>
> As a Hilbert space, $\mathbb{R}^{n}$ is defined with the [[2.1 - Vectors#^b212ec]] and the induced norm $\left\lvert{\mathbf{v}}\right\rvert^{2}=\mathbf{v}(\mathbf{v})$.
>$$
>\mathbb{R}^{n}\equiv\left\{\begin{bmatrix}
x_{1} \\ \vdots \\ x_{n}
\end{bmatrix}:x_{j\in \mathbb{Z}^{+}}\in \mathbb{R}\right\}$$
>

## Span

> [!def] Span
> The **span** of a set of vectors $\{\mathbf{v}_{n} \}$ is the set of *every possible [[2.1 - Vectors#^93b383]]* of its members.
>
>
> $$span\{\mathbf{v}_{n} \}\equiv\left\{ \sum_{n}{c_{n}\,\mathbf{v}_{n}:c_{n}\in \mathbb{R}}  \right\}$$

^d0ac06

> [!thm] Subspaces are Spans
> Let $V\subset \mathbb{R}^{n}$ be a [[#^420b21]]. There must exist vectors $\{\mathbf{u}_{i} \}$ that [[#^d0ac06]] $V$.
>
> $$V \text{ is a subspace of } \mathbb{R}^{n}\iff \exists \{\mathbf{u}_{i} \}\text{ such that } V=span\{\mathbf{u}_{i} \}$$
>

## Linear Independence

> [!def] Linearly Independent Set
> A [[2.0 - Sets#^742d66]] of vectors $\{\mathbf{v}_{1},\dots,\mathbf{v}_{n} \}$ is **linearly independent** if every $\mathbf{v}_{i}$ is *outside* the [[#^d0ac06]] of the other vectors $\{\mathbf{v}_{j} \}$ in the set.
>
> $$\{\mathbf{v}_{1},\dots,\mathbf{v}_{n} \}\text{ is linearly independent}\iff\mathbf{v}_{j}\notin span(\{\mathbf{v}_{j}:j\neq i \}), \ \forall i \neq j$$
>

^992f04

> [!thm] Linear Independence Test
> A set of vectors $\{\mathbf{v}_{1},\dots,\mathbf{v}_{n} \}$ is a [[#^992f04]] if the matrix $\begin{bmatrix}\mathbf{v}_{1}  & \dots & \mathbf{v}_{n}\end{bmatrix}$ has a [[2.4 - Determinants#^5da110]].
>
> $$\{\mathbf{v}_{1},\dots,\mathbf{v}_{n} \}\text{ is linearly independent}\iff\det(\begin{bmatrix}
\mathbf{v_1} & \dots & \mathbf{v_k}
\end{bmatrix})\neq0$$
>

## Basis

> [!def] Basis
> A **basis** $\{\mathbf{u}_{n} \}$ for a [[#^420b21]] $V$ must [[#^d0ac06]] $V$ and be a [[#^992f04]].
>
> $$V=span(\{\mathbf{u}_i \}) \text{ and }\{\mathbf{u}_{i}\}\text{ is linearly independent}\iff \{u_{i} \}\text{ is a basis for }V$$
>
> Any [[2.1 - Vector Spaces#^f36929]] $\mathbf{v}\in V$ can be written in basis $\{\mathbf{u}_{n} \}$ where the component $c_{n}$ is given by the [[2.1 - Vectors#^efaf9e]] $\mathbf{v}\cdot \mathbf{u}_{n}$.
>
> $$\mathbf{v}_{\{\mathbf{u}_{n} \}}=\sum_{k=1}^{n}{c_{k}\,\mathbf{u}_{k}}=\sum_{k=1}^{n}{(\mathbf{v}\cdot \mathbf{u}_{k})\,\mathbf{u}_{k}}$$

^ffe859

> [!def] Dimension
> The **dimension** of a subspace $V$ is it's number of [[#^ffe859]] vectors $\{\mathbf{u}_{i} \}$.
> $$dim(V)\equiv\lvert{\{\mathbf{u}_{i} \}}\rvert\,$$

^09a800

> [!thm] Exchange Theorem
> The size of a [[#^992f04]] $\{\mathbf{v}_{i} \}$ within a subspace $V$ must be less than or equal to the [[#^09a800]] of $V$.

### Orthogonality

> [!def] Orthogonal Basis
> An **orthogonal** [[2.2 - Subspaces#^ffe859]] is formed by a set of [[2.1 - Vectors#^2f7935]].

^0d2bd7

> [!def] Orthonormal Basis
> An **orthonormal** [[2.2 - Subspaces#^ffe859]] $\{\mathbf{u}_{i} \}$ is formed by a set of [[2.1 - Vectors#^452a2a]]. Let $\delta_{ij}$ be the [[3.5 - Step and Delta#^7d7ba6]] for indices $(i,j)$.
 > $$\braket{u_{i}|u_{j}}=\delta_{ij}$$

^c6eb2b

> [!def] Complete Orthonormal Basis
> An [[#^c6eb2b]] for a [[2.1 - Vector Spaces#^0af3a0]] is a **complete orthonormal basis**, expressed compactly with the [[2.1 - Vector Spaces#^3e433d]].
> 1. $$\braket{u_{i}|u_{j}}=\delta_{ij}$$
> 2. $$\sum_{i=0}^{n}\ket{u_{i}} \bra{u_{i}} =\mathbf{I}$$

### Standard Basis

> [!cor] Basis in Real Space
> Any [[#^992f04]] $\{ u_{1},\dots,u_{n}\}$ is a [[#^ffe859]] for $V\subset \mathbb{R}^{n}$.
> $$\{ u_{1},\dots,u_{n}\}\text{ is linearly independent}\iff\{ u_{1},\dots,u_{n}\} \text{ is a basis for } V\subset \mathbb{R}^{n} $$
>

> [!def] Standard Basis
> The $i$th standard [[2.2 - Subspaces#^c6eb2b]] $\mathbf{e}_{i}$ for the [[2.2 - Subspaces#^19a139]] $\mathbb{R}^{n}$ is the $i$th column of the [[2.2 - Matrices#^369c51]].
> $$\{\mathbf{e}_{1},\dots,\mathbf{e}_{n} \}=\left\{\begin{bmatrix}
1 \\ \vdots \\ 0
\end{bmatrix}\\,\dots,\begin{bmatrix}
0 \\ \vdots \\ 1
\end{bmatrix}\right\}$$
