# 2.1 - Vector Spaces

> [!def] Vector Space
> A **vector space** $V$ is a set of entities where *addition* and *scalar multiplication* are defined and a zero exists.
>
> For every [[#^f36929]] $(\mathbf{u},\mathbf{v},\mathbf{w})\in V$ and $(c,d)\in \mathbb{R}$,
>
> 1. $$\mathbf{u}+\mathbf{v}\in V$$
> 2. $$c\,\mathbf{u}\in V$$
> 3. $$\mathbf{u}+\mathbf{v} = \mathbf{v}+\mathbf{u}$$
> 4. $$(\mathbf{u}+\mathbf{v})+\mathbf{w} = \mathbf{u}+(\mathbf{v}+\mathbf{w})$$
> 5. $$0+\mathbf{u} = \mathbf{u}+0 = \mathbf{u}$$
> 6. $$(-\mathbf{u}) + \mathbf{u} = \mathbf{u} + (-\mathbf{u}) = 0$$
> 7. $$0\mathbf{u} = 0$$
> 8. $$1\mathbf{u} = \mathbf{u}$$
> 9. $$(cd)\mathbf{u} = c(d\mathbf{u})$$
> 10. $$c\,(\mathbf{u}+\mathbf{v}) = c\mathbf{u} + c\,\mathbf{v}$$
> 11. $$(c+d)\,\mathbf{u} = c\,\mathbf{u} +d\,\mathbf{u}$$

> [!def] Vector
> A **vector** $\mathbf{v}$ is an entity in a [[2.1 - Vector Spaces#^87cfc7]] $V$.

## Hilbert Spaces

> [!def] Hilbert Space
> A Hilbert Space $\mathcal{H}$ is an [[#^eccc66]] $V$ that is also a [[#^0af3a0]] with respect to the norm induced by the defined inner product.
>
> 1. $$\exists\braket{v_{1},v_{2}}\quad\forall \big(\Ket{v_{1}}, \ \Ket{v_{2}}  \big)\in \mathcal{H}$$
> 2. $$\mathcal{H} \text{ is complete with respect to } \left\lvert{v}\right\rvert^{2}=\Braket{v | v }   $$

^aece1f

> [!def] Inner Product Space
> A [[#^87cfc7]] $V$ with a defined [[2.1 - Vectors#^efaf9e]].
> $$\exists(\mathbf{v}_{1}\cdot \mathbf{v}_{2})\quad\forall (\mathbf{v}_{1},\mathbf{v}_{2})\in V$$

^eccc66

> [!def] Normed Space
> A [[#^87cfc7]] $V$ with a defined [[2.1 - Vectors#^57eeb7]].
> $$\exists\left\lvert{\mathbf{v}}\right\rvert\quad\forall \mathbf{v}\in V$$

### Completeness

> [!def] Complete Normed Space
> A [[#^706504]] is **complete** if every [[5.1 - Infinite Sequences#^42955f]] $\{a_n\}\subseteq V$ is also a [[5.1 - Infinite Sequences#^654999]] in $V$. Equivalently, $V$ equals its own [[2.1 - Vector Spaces#^8a53cb]] with respect to its defined norm.
> $$V=\overline{V}$$
>

^0af3a0

> [!def] Closure
> The closure $\bar{S}$ of a [[2.0 - Sets#^734f1e]] $S\subseteq X$ is the set of all points in the parent space $x\in X$ reachable as the [[3.4 - Limits#^1a2846]] of a [[5.1 - Infinite Sequences#^42955f]] $\{s_{n} \}\subseteq S$.
> $$\overline{S}=\{\lim_{n\to\infty}s_{n} : \{s_{n} \}\subseteq S \text{ and } \exists \lim_{n\to\infty}{} s_{n} \}$$

^8a53cb

> [!thm] Completeness Relation
> Given an [[2.2 - Subspaces#^c6eb2b]] $\{\Ket{v_{i}} \}$ of a [[#^aece1f]], the [[2.1 - Vectors#^4898e4]] of each $\Ket{v_{i}}$ with itself sums to the [[2.2 - Matrices#^369c51]].
>
> $$\sum_{i=1}^{n}\ket{v_{i}} \bra{v_{i}}  =\mathbf{I}$$
>
> For a continuous [[2.7 - Function Spaces#^549a88]], the completeness relation becomes as [[3.9 - Integrals#^integral]] equating to the [[2.7 - Function Spaces#^3fa82b]].
> $$\int \ket{x} \bra{x} \,dx=1$$
