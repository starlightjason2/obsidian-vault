# 3.1.1 - Scalar Functions

> [!def] Function
> A **scalar function** is a mapping $f(t_{1},\dots,t_{n}):\mathbb{R}^{n}\to \mathbb{R}$. Let the domain $D\subset \mathbb{R}^{n}$ be the input space, and the range $R\in \mathbb{R}$ be the output space. Functions live in a [[2.7 - Function Spaces#^549a88]].
>
> $$f(t_{1},\dots,t_{n})\equiv\{D\subset \mathbb{R}^{n}\} \to \{R\subset \mathbb{R} \}$$

>
>
>

> [!def] Scalar Field
> A **vector field** is a [[#^f874ea]] mapping each point in space to a scalar.
> $$f(x,y,z)=p$$

> [!def] Level Curves
> To visualize a [[#^f874ea]], we often use a contour map. For a 4D three variable function, the level "curve" will be a 3D level surface. Let $c$ be a constant.
> $$f(x,y, \dots)=c$$

## Boundary Conditions

> [!def] Homogeneous Boundary Conditions
> Homogeneous boundary conditions are constraints involving a [[#^f874ea]] $y(x)$ at $x=a$ and $x=b$ with zero on the right-hand side. The conditions may often also include the [[3.5 - Derivatives#^ea814a]] $y'(x)$.
> $$\begin{align}
y(a)&=0 \\
y(b)&=0
\end{align}$$

^2f00e2

> [!def] Dirichlet Boundary Conditions
> [[#^2f00e2]] that vanish at both endpoints:
> $$y(a)=0, \qquad y(b)=0$$

> [!def] Neumann Boundary Conditions
> [[#^2f00e2]] where $y'(x)$ vanishes at both endpoints:
> $$y'(a)=0, \qquad y'(b)=0$$

> [!def] Robin Boundary Conditions
> [[#^2f00e2]] where a [[2.1 - Vectors#^93b383]] of $y(x)$ and $y'(x)$ vanish at both endpoints:
> $$\alpha\, y(a)+\beta\, y'(a)=0, \qquad \alpha\, y(b)+\beta\, y'(b)=0$$

> [!def] Periodic Boundary Conditions
> [[#^2f00e2]] where $y(x)$ and its $y'(x)$ match at both endpoints:
> $$y(a)=y(b), \qquad y'(a)=y'(b)$$

## Planes

> [!thm] Plane
> A [[#^f874ea]] function of the form $z=ax+by+c$ results in a **plane.** Then $\mathbf{n}=\left<n_{x},n_{y},n_{z}\right>$ is a **normal [[2.1 - Vector Spaces#^f36929]]** orthogonal to the plane.
> $$z=-\frac{n_{x}}{n_{z}}x-\frac{n_{y}}{n_{z}}y+d$$

^ce2c08

> [!cor] Parallel Planes
> Let $P_{1},P_{2}$ be two planes in three dimensional space, and let $\mathbf{n}_{1},\mathbf{n}_{2}$ be their normal vectors.
>
> 1. If $\mathbf{n}_{2}\parallel \mathbf{n}_{2}$, then $P_{1}\parallel P_{2}$
> 2. If $\mathbf{n}_{1}\perp \mathbf{n}_{2}$, then $P_{1}\perp P_{2}$
> 3. If $\mathbf{n}_{2}$ intersects $\mathbf{n}_{2}$, then $P_{1}$ intersects $P_{2}$

> [!cor] Distance between a point and a plane
> Let there be a plane with a normal vector $\mathbf{n}$, and let $Q$ be a point on the plane. The distance between any point $P$ and the plane is the [[2.1 - Vectors#^0ab8b3]] of $\mathbf{QP}$ onto $\mathbf{n}$.
>
> $$\frac{\lVert{\mathbf{QP}\cdot \mathbf{n}}\rVert}{\lVert{\mathbf{n}}\rVert}$$

## Quadric Surfaces

A second order polynomial function results in a **quadric surface**.

| Shape                    | Equation                                                           | Trace (constant $x$) | Trace (constant $y$) | Trace (constant $z$) |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------- | -------------------- | -------------------- |
| Ellipsoid                | $$\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}+\frac{z^{2}}{c^{2}}=1$$  | Ellipse              | Ellipse              | Ellipse              |
| Hyperboloid (one sheet)  | $$\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}-\frac{z^{2}}{c^{2}}=1$$  | Hyperbola            | Hyperbola            | Ellipse              |
| Hyperboloid (two sheets) | $$-\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}-\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}+\frac{z^{2}}{c^{2}}=1$$ | Hyperbola            | Hyperbola            | Ellipse or nothing   |
| Elliptic Cone            | $$\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}-\frac{z^{2}}{c^{2}}=0$$  | Hyperbola            | Hyperbola            | Ellipse              |
| Elliptic Parabloid       | $$\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}-z=0$$                    | Parabola             | Parabola             | Ellipse              |
| Hyperbolic Parabloid     | $$\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}-\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}-z=0$$                    | Parabola             | Parabola             | Hyperbola            |
