# 4.0 - ODEs

> [!def] ODE
> An $n$th *rank* ordinary differential equation (ODE) is an equation containing derivatives up to the [[3.7 - Multiple Derivatives#^ea814a]].
>
> Let $y^{(i)}(x)$ be the $i$th [[3.5 - Derivatives#^ea814a]] of $y(x)$.
> $$\sum_{i=0}^{n}{p_{i}(x,y)\,y^{(i)}(x)}=f(x,y)$$

## Initial Value Problems

> [!def] Initial Value Problem
> Consider an [[#^f66176]] of rank $n$ involving derivatives of $y(x)$.
>
> An initial value problem of rank $n$ has specified **initial values** $(x_{k},y_{k})$ for each $k$th order derivative where $k\in[0,n-1]$.
>
> $$\Big\{ \big(x_{k},f^{(k)}(x_{k})\big) \Big\}=\{(x_{0},y_{0}, \ \dots \ , \{x_{n-1},y_{n-1} \} \}$$
>
>

^520eb9

> [!thm] Uniqueness and Existence Theorem
> Let there be an [[#^520eb9]] $y'=f(x,y), \ y(x_{0})=y_{0}$.
>
> Consider the region $R:\{a<x<b, \ c<y<d \}$ where $(x_{0}\\,y_{0})\in R$.
>
> 1. If a function $f$ exhibits [[3.4 - Limits#^44db3d]] on $R$, , then the IVP has at **least one solution** on some $x_{0}\in(a,b)$.
> 2. If $f$ and its [[3.5 - Derivatives#^7ced7c]] $f_{y}$ exhibit continuity on $R$, then the IVP has a **unique solution** on some $x_{0}\in(a,b)$.

## Separable ODE

> [!def] Separable ODE
> A **separable** [[4.0 - ODEs#^f66176]] has a product solution $y(x)$
>
> $$g(y)\, \frac{dy}{dx}=f(x)$$
>
>

### General Solution

> [!def] Implicit Solution
> An **implicit solution** of a [[#^f63924]] is an equation in $x$ and $y$. The solution comes from the **differential form** of the equation, with $dx$ and $dy$ separated on either side.
>
> $$\int g(y)\,dy=\int f(x)\,dx$$
>
