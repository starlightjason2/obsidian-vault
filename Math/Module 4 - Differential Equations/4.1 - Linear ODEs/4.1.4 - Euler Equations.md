# 4.1.4 - Euler Equations

## Euler Equations

> [!def] Euler ODE
> An **euler equation** is a [[4.1.2 - Second Order Linear ODEs#^554323]] of the form,
> $$ax^{2}\,y''+bx\,y'+cy=0$$

^538832

> [!def] Indicial Equation
> Given an [[#^538832]] of the form $a\,x^{2}\,y''+b\,x\,y'+c\,y=0$, the characteristic equation, or **indicial polynomial**, is defined as
>
> $$ar(r-1)+br+c=0$$

^f2bfaf

### General Solution

> [!thm] General Solution to Euler Equation
> Given the [[#^f2bfaf]], the general solution is determined by the roots $r_{1}, \ r_{2}$. There are three scenarios for two real, one real, and two complex roots.
> $$y(x)=\begin{cases}
c_{1}\,x^{r_{1}}+c_{2}\,x^{r_{2}} & (r_{1}\neq r_{2})\in\mathbb{R}\\
 c_{1}\,x^{r_{1}}+c_{2}\ln(x)\,x^{r_{1}} & (r_{1}=r_{2})\in \mathbb{C}\\
\end{cases}$$

^35099b

`\begin{proof}`@[[#^35099b]]
Let $r_{1}, \ r_{2}$ be roots to the [[#^f2bfaf]]. Use the [[4.1.0 - Linear ODEs#^dad60e]] for $y_{1}=x^{r_{1}}, \ y_{2}=x^{r_{2}}$.

$$
\begin{vmatrix}
y_{1} & y_{2} \\
y_{1}' & y_{2}'
\end{vmatrix}=(x^{r_{1}})(r_{2}\,x^{r_{2}-1})-(x^{r_{2}})(r_{2}\,x^{r_{1}-1})\neq 0
$$

`\end{proof}`
