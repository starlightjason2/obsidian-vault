# 4.2.1 - Bernoulli Equations

## Bernoulli Equations

> [!def] Bernoulli ODE
> A **Bernoulli Equation** is an [[4.0 - ODEs#^ode]] where for $r\notin[0,1]$,
>
> $$\frac{dy}{dx}+p(x)\,y=f(x)\,y^{r}$$

^bernoulli-ode

> [!thm] General Solution to Bernoulli Equation
> The solution to any [[#^bernoulli-ode]] can be found by multiplying both sides by $e^{\int p(x)\,dx}$ and doing a $u$-sub integral where $u=y\,e^{\int p(x)\,dx}$. The general solution is
>
> $$y(x)={e^{-\int p(x)\,dx}}\left( (1-r)\int e^{(1-r)\int p(x)\,dx}f(x)\,dx+C \right)^{\frac{1}{-r+1}}$$
