# 4.2.3 - Autonomous ODEs

## Autonomous ODEs

> [!def] Second Order Autonomous ODE
> A second order autonomous [[4.0 - ODEs#^f66176]] is of the form
>$$
>y''(x)=f\left(y,y'\right)
>$$
>

^f69f36

### General Solution

> [!def] Poincaré phase plane
> Let there be a [[#^f69f36]] of the form ${y''}+q(y\\,y')\,y'+p(y)=0$.
>
> Substitute $v=\dot{y}$. Then, $\ddot{y}=\dot{v}=\frac{dv}{dy}\frac{dy}{dt}=v \frac{dv}{dy}$.
>
> $$v\, \frac{dv}{dy}+q(y,v)\,v=-p(y)$$
>
