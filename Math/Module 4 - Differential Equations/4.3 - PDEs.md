# 4.3 - PDEs

> [!def] PDE
> An $n$th *rank* partial differential equation (PDE) is an equation containing derivatives up to the [[3.7 - Multiple Derivatives#^nth-partial-derivative]].
>

^pde

> [!def] Separable PDE
> A **separable** [[#^pde]] in $u(x,t)$ has a product solution $u(x,t)=X(x)\,T(t)$.

^separable-pde
