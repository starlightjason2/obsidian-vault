# 1.6 - Galaxies

> [!thm]
> The density of the galactic disk is given by this equation, where $r$ is the distance from the center of the galaxy, $r_{d}$ is the scale length of the disk, $z$ is the height above or below the disk, and $z_{d}$ is the scale height.
> $$\rho(r,z)=\rho_{0}\,e^{-(r/r_{d})-(\left\lvert{z}\right\rvert/z_{d})} $$
