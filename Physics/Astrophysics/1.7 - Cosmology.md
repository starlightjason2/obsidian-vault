# 1.7 - Cosmology

> [!thm] Hubble's Law
> The universe is expanding linearly but without a center. Let $H_{0}=\pu{(68\pm2)kms-1 Mpc-1}$ be Hubble's Constant.
>
>

> [!thm]
> Let $H$ be the time dependent Hubble parameter for the expansion of the universe.
> $$V\equiv \dot{R}=\frac{\dot{a}}{a}R\equiv HR$$

> [!thm] Friedmann Equation
> The Friedmann equation governs the expansion of the universe. Let $k$ be the curvature of the universe, where $kc^{2}=-\frac{2U}{mR_{0}^{2}}$. If $U>0$, the universe is negatively curved. If $U<0$, the universe is positively curved.
> $$\left( \frac{\dot{a}}{a} \right)^{2}=\frac{8\pi G}{3}\rho+\frac{kc^{2}}{a^{2}}$$

> [!cor]
> As the universe expands, the energy density in cube with length $d$ and volume $V$ scales by factor $a$. By the [[#^434a62]], $a\propto \sqrt{t}$.
> $$\rho\equiv \frac{E}{d^{3}}\propto \frac{1}{a^{3}}$$
>

> [!def] Cosmological Constant
> Dark energy is continuously created at a rate proportional to volume. Plugging into the [[#^434a62]],
> $$a\propto e^{H_{0}t}$$
