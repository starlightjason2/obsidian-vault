# 4.5 - Damped Waves

> [!def] Damped Wave Equation in 1D
> The [[4.4 - Wave Equation#^aeb7c2]] with an additional [[1.11 - Drag Forces#^ec8e89]] $F_{d}=-\Gamma \,\dot{\psi}$ where $\Gamma \left[\pu{Hz}\right]$ is the *damping coefficient*.
> $$\frac{\partial^2 \psi}{\partial t^2}+\Gamma\, \frac{\partial \psi}{\partial t}-c^{2}\,\frac{\partial^2 \psi}{\partial x^2}=0$$

^d1a2b3

> [!def] Damped 1D Wave
> A damped [[4.4 - Wave Equation#^aeb7c2|1D Wave Equation]] obeys the [[#^d1a2b3]]. Using separation of variables $\psi(x,t)=X(x)\,T(t)$, the spatial part remains a [[4.1 - Simple Harmonic Oscillation#^d9cde8]] while the temporal part obeys a [[4.2 - Damped Harmonic Oscillation#^a1b2c3]] with $\beta=\Gamma/2$.
>
> The solution has three cases for [[#^57d8dc]], [[#^739494]], and [[#^4511ae]].
>
> $$\psi(x,t) = \begin{cases}  
A\,e^{-\Gamma t/2}\cos(kx-\omega_1 t + \phi) & \frac{\Gamma}{2} < \omega_0 \\[0.5em]
e^{-\Gamma t/2}(c_1 + c_2\,t)\cos(kx+\phi) & \frac{\Gamma}{2} = \omega_0 \\[0.5em]
(c_1\,e^{-\gamma_+ t} + c_2\,e^{-\gamma_- t})\cos(kx+\phi) & \frac{\Gamma}{2} > \omega_{0} \end{cases}$$

^e649e7

`\begin{proof}`@[[#^e649e7]]
Try the Ansatz $\psi(x,t)=X(x)\,T(t)$. Substituting into the [[#^d1a2b3]] and dividing by $X(x)\,T(t)$,

$$\frac{1}{T}\ddot{T}+\frac{\Gamma}{T}\dot{T}=\frac{c^{2}}{X}X''=-k^{2}c^{2}=-\omega_{0}^{2}$$

The spatial part is a [[4.1 - Simple Harmonic Oscillation#^6f684f]] with solution $X(x)=A\cos(kx+\phi)$. The temporal part is

$$\ddot{T}+\Gamma\,\dot{T}+\omega_{0}^{2}\,T=0$$

which is a [[4.2 - Damped Harmonic Oscillation#^a1b2c3]] with $\beta=\Gamma/2$. The three cases follow directly.
`\end{proof}`

## Underdamped Case

> [!thm] Underdamped Wave
> A [[#^e649e7]] where $\Gamma/2 < \omega_{0}$. The resulting motion is a *damped* travelling wave with damped frequency $\omega_{1}=\sqrt{\omega_{0}^{2}-\Gamma^{2}/4}$.
>
> $$\psi(x,t)=A\,e^{-\Gamma t/2}\cos(kx-\omega_{1}t+\phi) \quad;\quad \frac{\Gamma}{2}<\omega_{0}$$

^57d8dc

`\begin{proof}`@[[#^57d8dc]]
The temporal characteristic equation $r^{2}+\Gamma\,r+\omega_{0}^{2}=0$ has roots $r_{1,2}=-\frac{\Gamma}{2}\pm i\sqrt{\omega_{0}^{2}-\Gamma^{2}/4}$. Let $\omega_{1}=\sqrt{\omega_{0}^{2}-\Gamma^{2}/4}$. The temporal part is

$$T(t)=e^{-\Gamma t/2}(c_{1}\,e^{i\omega_{1}t}+c_{2}\,e^{-i\omega_{1}t})$$

Substitute a [[4.1 - Simple Harmonic Oscillation#^d9cde8]] for $\omega=\omega_{1}$ and combine with $X(x)$.
`\end{proof}`

## Critically Damped Case

> [!thm] Critically Damped Wave
> A [[#^e649e7]] where $\Gamma/2 = \omega_{0}$. The resulting motion is
>
> $$\psi(x,t)=e^{-\Gamma t/2}(c_{1}+c_{2}\,t)\cos(kx+\phi) \quad;\quad \frac{\Gamma}{2}=\omega_{0}$$

^739494

`\begin{proof}`@[[#^739494]]
The temporal characteristic equation $r^{2}+\Gamma\,r+\omega_{0}^{2}=0$ has a repeated root $r=-\Gamma/2$. By the [[4.1.3 - Constant Coefficient ODEs#^382f29]] (one root case),

$$T(t)=e^{-\Gamma t/2}(c_{1}+c_{2}\,t)$$

Combine with $X(x)$.
`\end{proof}`

## Overdamped Case

> [!thm] Overdamped Wave
> A [[#^e649e7]] where $\Gamma/2 > \omega_{0}$. Let $\gamma_{\pm}=\frac{\Gamma}{2}\pm\sqrt{\Gamma^{2}/4-\omega_{0}^{2}}$. The resulting motion is
>
> $$\psi(x,t)=(c_{1}\,e^{-\gamma_{+}t}+c_{2}\,e^{-\gamma_{-}t})\cos(kx+\phi) \quad;\quad \frac{\Gamma}{2}>\omega_{0}$$

^4511ae

`\begin{proof}`@[[#^4511ae]]
The temporal characteristic equation $r^{2}+\Gamma\,r+\omega_{0}^{2}=0$ has roots $r_{1,2}=-\frac{\Gamma}{2}\pm\sqrt{\Gamma^{2}/4-\omega_{0}^{2}}$, both real and negative. Let $r_{1,2}=-\gamma_{\pm}$ and substitute into the [[4.1.3 - Constant Coefficient ODEs#^382f29]] (two root case).

$$T(t)=c_{1}\,e^{-\gamma_{+}t}+c_{2}\,e^{-\gamma_{-}t}$$

Combine with $X(x)$.
`\end{proof}`
