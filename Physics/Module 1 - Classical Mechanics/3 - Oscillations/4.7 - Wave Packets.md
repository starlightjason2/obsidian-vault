# 4.7 - Wave Packets

> [!def] Wave Packet
> A cluster of [[4.4 - Plane Waves#^plane-wave|Travelling Waves]] $\psi(x,t)$ localized in space, moving as a single envelope. A superposition of sinusoidal waves produces constructive interference in a localized region and destructive interference elsewhere.
>
> The amplitude spectrum $g(k)$ gives the contribution of each wavenumber and is the [[3.7 - Periodic Functions#^fourier-transform]] of the initial profile: $\mathcal{F}\left\{ \psi(x,0)\right\}=g(k)$. The time evolution is then the modified [[3.7 - Periodic Functions#^inverse-fourier-transform]]:
>
> $$\psi(x,t)=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}g(k)\,e^{i(kx-\omega t)}\,dk, \qquad g(k)=\mathcal{F}\Big\{ \psi(x,0)\Big\}$$

^wave-packet

> [!def] Beat
> A [[#^wave-packet|Wave Packet]] formed by superposing two [[4.4 - Plane Waves#^plane-wave|Travelling Waves]] of nearly equal wavenumber and frequency. With $k_\text{avg}=(k_1+k_2)/2$ and $\Delta k=(k_1-k_2)/2$, the superposition factors into a carrier modulated by a slow envelope:
> $$\psi(x,t)=2A\cos(k_\text{avg}x-\omega_\text{avg}t)\cos(\Delta k\,x-\Delta\omega\,t)$$

^beat

> [!def] Group Velocity
> The group velocity is the speed of the packet envelope, and is the physically observable velocity:
> $$v_g=\frac{d\omega}{dk}$$
>

^group-velocity

## Examples

> [!example] Dispersion of EM Waves
> For an EM [[#^wave-packet|Wave Packet]] with [[4.4 - Plane Waves#^dispersion-relation|Dispersion Relation]] $\omega=ck$:
> $$v_\phi=v_g=c$$
> EM waves are non-dispersive in free space, and the packet $\psi(x,t)=\psi(x-ct)$ travels without changing shape.

^dispersion-of-em-waves
