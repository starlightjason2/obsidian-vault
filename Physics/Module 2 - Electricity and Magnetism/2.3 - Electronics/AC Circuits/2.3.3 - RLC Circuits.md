# 2.3.3 - RLC Circuits

## Band Pass Filters

> [!thm] Series RLC Circuit
> A series RLC circuit has an inductor with [[2.3.4 - Inductors#^f67d94]] $L$, resistor with [[2.3.1 - Ohm's Law#^995af2]] $R$, and capacitor with [[2.3.3 - Capacitors#^3b3908]] $C$, driven by external [[2.3.4 - AC Circuits#^a9d4a9]] $V_{E}(t)=V_{0}\,e^{i\omega t}$.
>
> The charge is a [[4.3 - Driven Harmonic Oscillation#^5cc7c5]] $q(t)=\left\lvert{q}\right\rvert \, e^{i(\omega t+\phi)}$ with damping factor $\beta=R/2L$ and resonant frequency $\omega_{0}=1/\sqrt{LC}$.
>
>$$\begin{align}  \\
\left\lvert{q}\right\rvert &=\frac{V_{0}/L}{\sqrt{(\omega_{0}^{2}-\omega ^{2})^{2}+4\beta^{2}\omega ^{2}}} , &
 \phi=\tan^{-1}\left( \frac{-2\beta \omega }{\omega_{0}^{2}-\omega ^{2}} \right) \\
\end{align}$$
>
> The [[2.3.4 - AC Circuits#^dba19d]] in the circuit is $I(t)=(\omega\left\lvert{q}\right\rvert/L) \,V_{E}$, where current phase $\phi_{I}=\phi+ \pi/2$. Each voltage $V_{L}, \ V_{R}, \ V_{C}$ is:
>$$\begin{align}  
V_{L}&= \omega^{2}L\left\lvert{q}\right\rvert\,e^{i(\omega t+\phi+\pi)} \\[0.5em]
V_{R}&=\omega R\left\lvert{q}\right\rvert\,e^{i(\omega t+\phi + \pi/2)} \\[0.5em]
V_{C}&=\frac{\left\lvert{q}\right\rvert}{C} \,e^{i(\omega t+\phi)}
\end{align}$$
>

^35d0b2

`\begin{proof}`@[[#^35d0b2]]
Let $q(t)$ be circuit charge. An equation of motion is given by [[2.3.1 - Ohm's Law#^03a977]], for voltage drop across the resistor ($V_{R}=IR=\dot{q}R$), inductor ($V_{L}=\ddot{q}$), and capacitor ($V_{C}=q/C$). The solution is a [[4.3 - Driven Harmonic Oscillation#^744bdf]].

$$
\begin{align}
\frac{V_{0}}{L}\,e^{i\omega t}&=\ddot{q}+\frac{L}{R}\,\dot{q}+\frac{1}{LC}\,q  \\[1em]
q(t) &=\frac{V_{0} \,e^{i(\omega t+\delta)}}{L\,\sqrt{(\omega_{0}^{2}-\omega ^{2})^{2}+4\beta^{2}\omega ^{2}}}
\end{align}
$$

Current is the time derivative of charge:

$$
\begin{align}
I(t)&=i\omega\left\lvert{q}\right\rvert\,e^{i\delta}\,e^{i\omega t}=\omega\left\lvert{q}\right\rvert\,e^{i(\delta+\pi/2)}\,e^{i\omega t} \\
&=\frac{\omega\,V_{0}  / L}{\sqrt{(\omega_{0}^{2}-\omega ^{2})^{2}+4\beta^{2}\omega ^{2}}}\,e^{i\phi_{I}}\,e^{i\omega t}
\end{align}
$$

Recognize the external [[2.3.4 - AC Circuits#^a9d4a9]] $V_{E}=V_{0}\,e^{i\omega t}$,

$$
I(t)=\frac{\,e^{i\phi_{I}}\,\omega  / L}{\sqrt{(\omega_{0}^{2}-\omega ^{2})^{2}+4\beta^{2}\omega ^{2}}}\,V_{E}
$$

> [!thm] Admittance in Series RLC
> The [[2.3.4 - AC Circuits#^1ae872]] in a [[#^35d0b2]] is
> $$Y=\frac{V_{R}}{V_{E}} \frac{1}{R}=\frac{e^{i\phi_{I}}\,\omega  / L}{\sqrt{(\omega_{0}^{2}-\omega ^{2})^{2}+4\beta^{2}\omega ^{2}}}$$
>
>

^nosaj5

`\begin{proof}`@[[#^nosaj5]]
By Ohm's Law, $V_{E}=I(t) /Y$ and $V_{R}=I(t)R$, where $Y$ is the [[2.3.4 - AC Circuits#^1ae872]]. Solving for $Y$,

$$
Y=\frac{V_{R}}{V_{E}} \frac{1}{R}=\frac{e^{i\phi_{I}}\,\omega  / L}{\sqrt{(\omega_{0}^{2}-\omega ^{2})^{2}+4\beta^{2}\omega ^{2}}}
$$

`\end{proof}`

## High Pass Filters

A **high-pass filter** allows high frequencies to pass while blocking low frequencies. The output is taken across the element with impedance that *increases* with frequency.

### CR Circuit

> [!def] Cutoff Frequency
> $$\omega_c = \frac{1}{RC}$$

> [!def] Admittance
> The admittance $A(\omega)$ for a CR high-pass filter with [[2.3.3 - Capacitors#^3b3908]] $C$ and [[2.3.1 - Ohm's Law#^995af2]] $R$:
> $$\left|A(\omega)\right| = \left|\frac{V_{\text{out}}}{V_{\text{in}}}\right| = \frac{\omega CR}{\sqrt{1 + \omega^2 R^2 C^2}} = \frac{\omega/\omega_c}{\sqrt{1 + (\omega/\omega_c)^2}}$$

^29c120

By voltage divider with $Z_C = (i\omega C)^{-1}$, output across $R$:

$$\frac{V_{\text{out}}}{V_{\text{in}}} = \frac{Z_R}{Z_C + Z_R} = \frac{R}{\frac{1}{i\omega C} + R} = \frac{i\omega CR}{1 + i\omega CR}$$

> [!thm] Phase
> $$\phi = \tan^{-1}(\omega CR)$$
> From [[3.6 - Complex Numbers#^88e10c]] on $\frac{i\omega CR}{1 + i\omega CR}$.

### LR Circuit

> [!def] Cutoff Frequency
> $$\omega_c = \frac{R}{L}$$

> [!def] Admittance
> The admittance $A(\omega)$ for an LR high-pass filter with [[2.3.4 - Inductors#^f67d94]] $L$ and [[2.3.1 - Ohm's Law#^995af2]] $R$:
> $$\left|A(\omega)\right| = \frac{\omega L}{\sqrt{R^{2}+\omega^{2}L^{2}}} = \frac{\omega/\omega_c}{\sqrt{1 + (\omega/\omega_c)^2}}$$

By voltage divider, output across $L$:

$$\frac{V_{\text{out}}}{V_{\text{in}}} = \frac{Z_L}{Z_R + Z_L} = \frac{i\omega L}{R + i\omega L}$$

## Low Pass Filters

A **low-pass filter** allows low frequencies to pass while blocking high frequencies. The output is taken across the element with impedance that *decreases* with frequency.

### RC Circuit

> [!def] Cutoff Frequency of RC Circuit
> $$\omega_c = \frac{1}{RC}$$

> [!def] Admittance
> The admittance $A(\omega)$ for an RC low-pass filter with [[2.3.1 - Ohm's Law#^995af2]] $R$ and [[2.3.3 - Capacitors#^3b3908]] $C$:
> $$\left|A(\omega)\right| = \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 + \omega^2 R^2 C^2}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 + (\omega/\omega_c)^2}}$$

By voltage divider, output across $C$:

$$\frac{V_{\text{out}}}{V_{\text{in}}} = \frac{Z_C}{Z_R + Z_C} = \frac{\frac{1}{i\omega C}}{R + \frac{1}{i\omega C}} = \frac{1}{1 + i\omega RC}$$

When the pulse width is $\Delta t \ll \tau = RC$, we have $q(t) \approx 0$. There's little time to build up charge on the [[2.3.3 - Capacitors#^3b3908]].

$$\begin{align}
q(t) &\approx \int \frac{V_{\text{in}}}{R}\,dt \\
V_{\text{out}} = \frac{q}{C} &= \frac{1}{RC}\int V_{\text{in}}\,dt
\end{align}$$

### RL Circuit

> [!def] Cutoff Frequency of RL Circuit
> $$\omega_c = \frac{R}{L}$$

> [!def] Admittance
> The admittance $A(\omega)$ for an RL low-pass filter with [[2.3.1 - Ohm's Law#^995af2]] $R$ and [[2.3.4 - Inductors#^f67d94]] $L$:
> $$\left|A(\omega)\right| = \frac{R}{\sqrt{R^{2}+\omega^{2}L^{2}}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 + (\omega/\omega_c)^2}}$$

By voltage divider, output across $R$:

$$\frac{V_{\text{out}}}{V_{\text{in}}} = \frac{Z_R}{Z_R + Z_L} = \frac{R}{R + i\omega L}$$
