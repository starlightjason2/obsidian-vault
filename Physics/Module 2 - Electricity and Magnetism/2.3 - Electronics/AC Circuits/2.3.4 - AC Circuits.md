## Impedance

> [!thm] Impedance
> Impedance $Z$ is a [[3.6 - Complex Numbers#^4b0f81]] characterizing generalized AC resistance, with real part being [[2.3.1 - Ohm's Law#^995af2]] $R$ and imaginary part being [[#^d6721a]] $X$.
> $$Z=R+i\,X$$

^18951f

> [!def] Reactance
> The imaginary component of complex impedance.

### Inductors

> [!def] Inductor Impedance
> The [[2.3.4 - AC Circuits#^18951f]] for an inductor with [[2.3.4 - Inductors#^f67d94]] $L$ is
> $$Z_{L}=i\omega L$$

^263d5b

`\begin{proof}`[[#^263d5b]]
Assume $I(t)=I_{0}e^{i\omega t}$. Then, with [[2.3.4 - AC Circuits#^986cfb]] $V=IZ_{L}$,

 $$Z_{L}=\frac{V_{L}}{I}=\frac{L\dot{I}}{I}=\frac{L(i\omega I_{0}e^{i\omega t})}{I}=i\omega L$$
`\end{proof}`

> [!thm] Admittance
> Admittance $Y$ is a [[3.6 - Complex Numbers#^4b0f81]] characterizing how easily AC current will flow. It's the inverse of [[#^18951f]] $Z$.
> $$Y\equiv \frac{1}{Z}$$

> [!thm] Bode Plot
> Using [[2.1.2 - Electric Potential#^aed0e0]] $P = V^2/R$ and letting $A(\omega)$ be the [[2.3.3 - RLC Circuits#^29c120]]:
> $$\text{dB} = 10\log_{10}\left(\frac{P_{\text{out}}}{P_{\text{in}}}\right) = 20\log_{10}|A(\omega)|$$
