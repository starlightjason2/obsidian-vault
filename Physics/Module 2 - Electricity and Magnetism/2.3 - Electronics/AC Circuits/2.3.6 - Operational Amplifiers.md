# 2.3.6 - Operational Amplifiers

## Open Loop

> [!def] Open Loop Op-Amp Gain
> An Op-amp amplifies and compares [[2.3.1 - Ohm's Law#^voltage]] $V_{in}=\left\{V_{+}, \ V_{-} \right\}$ and [[2.1.4 - Current#^current]] $I_{in}$ and do comparative operations. Let $G_{0}$ be the gain constant. It responds instantaneously at some voltage 
> $$V_{out}=G_{0}(V_{+}-V_{-})$$

> [!def]
> $$\beta=\frac{I_{C}}{I_{B}}$$

## Closed Loop

> [!def] Closed Loop Op-Amp Gain
> $$\begin{align}
G&=\frac{V_{in}}{V_{out}}=1 \\
&=\frac{1}{\beta}=1+ \frac{R_{2}}{R_{1}}
\end{align}$$

### Golden Rules

> [!thm] Golden Rules for Closed Loop Op-amps
> The output $V_{out}$ will adjust such that,
> 1. $$I_{in}=0$$
> 2. $$V_{+}\approxeq V_{-}$$

## Inverting

> [!def] Non-Inverting Op-amp Gain
> Consider a closed loop Op-amp where the ground $V_{-}$ is connected to the output $V_{out}$. Let $R_{2}, \ R_{1}$ be the [[2.3.1 - Ohm's Law#^resistance]].
> $$G=\frac{1}{\beta}=1+ \frac{R_{2}}{R_{1}}$$

> [!def] Inverting Op-amp Gain
> Consider a closed loop Op-amp where by [[2.3.1 - Ohm's Law#^ohms-law]], $V_{in}-V_{out}=IR_{1}$ for $V_{+}=\pu{0V}\approxeq V_{-}$. Let $R_{2}, \ R_{1}$ be the [[2.3.1 - Ohm's Law#^resistance]] and $V_{in}-V_{out}=\frac{V_{in}}{R_{2}}(R_{1}+R_{2})$
> $$G=\frac{V_{out}}{V_{in}}=-\frac{R_{2}}{R_{1}}$$

### Transimpedance Amp

> [!def] Inverting Op-amp Gain
> Consider an Op-amp where by [[2.3.1 - Ohm's Law#^ohms-law]], $V_{in}-V_{out}=IR_{1}$ for $V_{in}=\pu{0V}$. Let $R_{2}, \ R_{1}$ be the [[2.3.1 - Ohm's Law#^resistance]].
> $$I=-\frac{V_{out}}{R_{1}}$$
