# 2.3.2 - Diodes

## Diodes

> [!def] Diode
> The [[2.1.4 - Current#^45defa]] $I$ across a **diode** is non-ohmic, and exponentially decays with the [[2.3.1 - Ohm's Law#^23952c]] $V$. Let $V_{T}\approx \pu{0.07V}$ be the *thermal voltage*.
> $$I=I_{0}(e^{V/V_{T}}-1)$$

### Transistors

> [!def] Transistor
> A transistor is a switch consisting of [[#^f1d0c6]] base-collector and base-emitter with currents $I_{B}$, $I_{E}$, $I_{C}$. When off, each diode is oriented opposite and no current flows. When $V_{B}\approx \pu{0.7V}$ is applied to the base, current runs from the collector to the emitter, amplified by the [[#^e4f834]].
>
> By [[2.3.1 - Ohm's Law#^03a977]],
> $$I_{E}=I_{C}+I_{B}$$

^561c6a

> [!def] Transistor Gain
> A [[#^561c6a]]'s emitter current is the base current amplified by a *gain factor* $\beta$.
> $$\beta\equiv\frac{I_{C}}{I_{B}}\approx300$$
