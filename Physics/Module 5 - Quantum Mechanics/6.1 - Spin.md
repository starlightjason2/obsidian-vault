# 6.1 - Spin

> [!def] Spin
> A quantized property of a particle analogous to [[1.8 - Angular Momentum#^angular-momentum]].

^spin

## Spin 1/2

> [!def] Spin 1/2 Observable
> In [[2.6 - Coordinate Sytems#^cartesian-coordinates]], by convention, we use the [[2.6 - Eigenvectors and Eigenvalues#^eigenbasis]] of [[#^spin-12-along-z]] to write the [[#^spin]] [[6.0 - Postulates#^discrete-observable]] $\mathbf{\hat{S}}$.
> $$\begin{align}
\Ket{+\frac{1}{2}}_{z} \doteq\begin{bmatrix}
1 \\
0
\end{bmatrix}, \\
\Ket{-\frac{1}{2}}_{z} \doteq\begin{bmatrix}
0 \\
1
\end{bmatrix}
\end{align}$$

^d01b00

### Z Axis

> [!thm] Spin 1/2 along $z$
> The [[#^d01b00]] $\mathbf{\hat{S}}_{z}$ along the $z$ axis. The $z$ subscript is often dropped by convention.
>
> $$ \mathbf{\hat{S}}_{z} = \frac{\hbar}{2}\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & -1 \end{bmatrix}$$

^spin-12-along-z

> [!cor] Eigenstates of $\mathbf{\hat{S}}_z$ for Spin-1/2
> The [[2.7 - Function Spaces#^eigenfunction]] of [[#^spin-12-along-z]] $\mathbf{\hat{S}}_z$ is
>
> $$\mathbf{\hat{S}}_z\Ket{+\frac{1}{2}}_z = \frac{\hbar}{2}\Ket{+\frac{1}{2}}_z, \quad \mathbf{\hat{S}}_z\Ket{-\frac{1}{2}}_z = -\frac{\hbar}{2}\Ket{-\frac{1}{2}}_z$$
>
>

### X Axis

> [!thm] Spin 1/2 along $x$
> The [[#^d01b00]] $\mathbf{\hat{S}}_{x}$ along the $x$ axis.
>
> $$\mathbf{\hat{S}}_{x} = \frac{\hbar}{2}\begin{bmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{bmatrix}$$

^spin-12-along-x

> [!cor] Eigenstates of $\mathbf{\hat{S}}_x$ for Spin-1/2
> The [[2.7 - Function Spaces#^eigenfunction]] of [[#^spin-12-along-x]] $\mathbf{\hat{S}}_x$ with eigenvalues $\pm\frac{\hbar}{2}$:
>
> $$\Ket{+\frac{1}{2}}_x = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}, \quad \Ket{-\frac{1}{2}}_x = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ -1 \end{bmatrix}$$

### Y Axis

> [!thm] Spin 1/2 along $y$
> The [[#^d01b00]] $\mathbf{\hat{S}}_{y}$ along the $y$ axis.
>
> $$\mathbf{\hat{S}}_{y}= \frac{\hbar}{2}\begin{bmatrix} 0 & -i \\ i & 0 \end{bmatrix}$$

^spin-12-along-y

> [!cor] Eigenstates of $\mathbf{\hat{S}}_y$ for Spin-1/2
> The [[2.7 - Function Spaces#^eigenfunction]] of [[#^spin-12-along-y]] $\mathbf{\hat{S}}_y$ with eigenvalues $\pm\frac{\hbar}{2}$:
>
> $$\Ket{+\frac{1}{2}}_y = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ i \end{bmatrix}, \quad \Ket{-\frac{1}{2}}_y = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ -i \end{bmatrix}$$

### General Direction

> [!thm] Spin 1/2 along $\hat{\boldsymbol{n}}$
> The [[#^d01b00]] $\mathbf{\hat{S}}_{n}$ along an arbitrary direction $\hat{\boldsymbol{n}}$, where in [[2.6 - Coordinate Sytems#^spherical-coordinates]] $(\phi,\theta)$, the direction $\hat{\boldsymbol{n}}=\sin\theta \cos \phi\,\hat{\boldsymbol{x}}+\sin\theta \sin \phi \,\hat{\boldsymbol{y}}+\cos\theta\,\hat{\boldsymbol{z}}$.
>
> $$\mathbf{\hat{S}}_{n}= \frac{\hbar}{2}\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta & \sin\theta\,e^{-i\phi} \\ \sin\theta\,e^{i\phi} & -\cos\theta \end{bmatrix}$$

^spin-12-along-hatboldsymboln

> [!cor] Eigenstates of $\mathbf{\hat{S}}_n$ for Spin-1/2
> The [[2.7 - Function Spaces#^eigenfunction]] of [[#^spin-12-along-hatboldsymboln]] $\mathbf{\hat{S}}_n$ with eigenvalues $\pm\frac{\hbar}{2}$:
>
> $$\Ket{+\frac{1}{2}}_n = \begin{bmatrix} \cos\left(\frac{\theta}{2}\right) \\ \sin\left(\frac{\theta}{2}\right)e^{i\phi} \end{bmatrix}, \quad \Ket{-\frac{1}{2}}_n = \begin{bmatrix} -\sin\left(\frac{\theta}{2}\right) \\ \cos\left(\frac{\theta}{2}\right)e^{i\phi} \end{bmatrix}$$

### Commutation Relations

> [!thm] Spin-1/2 Commutators
> The [[2.2 - Special Matrices#^commutator]] of the spin-1/2 operators satisfy the angular momentum commutation relations:
>
> $$[\mathbf{\hat{S}}_x, \mathbf{\hat{S}}_y] = i\hbar\mathbf{\hat{S}}_z, \quad [\mathbf{\hat{S}}_y, \mathbf{\hat{S}}_z] = i\hbar\mathbf{\hat{S}}_x, \quad [\mathbf{\hat{S}}_z, \mathbf{\hat{S}}_x] = i\hbar\mathbf{\hat{S}}_y$$
>
>

^spin-12-commutators

> [!cor] Non-Simultaneous Measurements for Spin-1/2
> Since the spin operators do not [[2.2 - Special Matrices#^commuting-matrices]], by [[6.0 - Postulates#^commuting-operators-share-eigenstates]], different spin components cannot be measured simultaneously. Measuring one component disturbs the system and affects subsequent measurements of other components.

### Expectation Values

> [!thm] Expectation Values for Spin-1/2
> For a spin-1/2 system in state $\Ket{\psi}$, the [[6.2 - Uncertainty#^expectation-value]] of spin measurements along any axis are bounded:
>
> $$-\frac{\hbar}{2} \leq \langle \mathbf{\hat{S}}_i \rangle \leq \frac{\hbar}{2} \quad \text{for } i \in \{x, y, z\}$$
>
> For a particle in eigenstate $\Ket{\pm\frac{1}{2}}_z$:
> $$\langle \mathbf{\hat{S}}_z \rangle = \pm\frac{\hbar}{2}, \quad \langle \mathbf{\hat{S}}_x \rangle = 0, \quad \langle \mathbf{\hat{S}}_y \rangle = 0$$

^expectation-values-for-spin-12

### Uncertainty Relations

> [!thm] Uncertainty for Spin-1/2
> By the [[6.2 - Uncertainty#^uncertainty]] Uncertainty Principle, for any two orthogonal spin components:
>
> $$\Delta \mathbf{\hat{S}}_x \, \Delta \mathbf{\hat{S}}_y \geq \frac{1}{2}\left|\langle [\mathbf{\hat{S}}_x, \mathbf{\hat{S}}_y] \rangle\right| = \frac{\hbar}{2}\left|\langle \mathbf{\hat{S}}_z \rangle\right|$$
>
> For a particle in eigenstate $\Ket{\pm\frac{1}{2}}_z$:
> $$\Delta \mathbf{\hat{S}}_z = 0, \quad \Delta \mathbf{\hat{S}}_x = \frac{\hbar}{2}, \quad \Delta \mathbf{\hat{S}}_y = \frac{\hbar}{2}$$
>
> and thus: $\Delta \mathbf{\hat{S}}_x \, \Delta \mathbf{\hat{S}}_y = \frac{\hbar^2}{4}$

^uncertainty-for-spin-12

## Spin 1

> [!def] Spin-1 Observable
> In [[2.6 - Coordinate Sytems#^cartesian-coordinates]], by convention, we use the [[2.6 - Eigenvectors and Eigenvalues#^eigenbasis]] of [[#^spin-1-along-z]] to write the [[#^spin]] [[6.0 - Postulates#^discrete-observable]] $\mathbf{\hat{S}}$.
> $$\Ket{1}_z \doteq \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix}, \quad \Ket{0}_z \doteq \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 1 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix}, \quad \Ket{-1}_z \doteq \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 0 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}$$

^spin-1-observable

### Z Axis

> [!thm] Spin-1 along $z$
> The [[#^spin-1-observable]] $\mathbf{\hat{S}}_z$ along the $z$ axis.
>
> $$\mathbf{\hat{S}}_z = \hbar\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & -1 \end{bmatrix}$$

^spin-1-along-z

> [!cor] Eigenstates of $\mathbf{\hat{S}}_z$ for Spin-1
> The [[2.7 - Function Spaces#^eigenfunction]] of [[#^spin-1-along-z]] $\mathbf{\hat{S}}_z$ is
>
> $$\mathbf{\hat{S}}_z\Ket{1}_z = \hbar\Ket{1}_z, \quad \mathbf{\hat{S}}_z\Ket{0}_z = 0\Ket{0}_z, \quad \mathbf{\hat{S}}_z\Ket{-1}_z = -\hbar\Ket{-1}_z$$
>
>

### X Axis

> [!thm] Spin-1 along $x$
> The [[#^spin-1-observable]] $\mathbf{\hat{S}}_x$ along the $x$ axis.
>
> $$\mathbf{\hat{S}}_x = \frac{\hbar}{\sqrt{2}}\begin{bmatrix} 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \end{bmatrix}$$

^spin-1-along-x

> [!cor] Eigenstates of $\mathbf{\hat{S}}_x$ for Spin-1
> The [[2.7 - Function Spaces#^eigenfunction]] of [[#^spin-1-along-x]] $\mathbf{\hat{S}}_x$ with eigenvalues $\hbar, 0, -\hbar$:
>
> $$\Ket{1}_x = \frac{1}{2}\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ \sqrt{2} \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}, \quad \Ket{0}_x = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \\ -1 \end{bmatrix}, \quad \Ket{-1}_x = \frac{1}{2}\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ -\sqrt{2} \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}$$

### Y Axis

> [!thm] Spin-1 along $y$
> The [[#^spin-1-observable]] $\mathbf{\hat{S}}_y$ along the $y$ axis.
>
> $$\mathbf{\hat{S}}_y = \frac{\hbar}{\sqrt{2}}\begin{bmatrix} 0 & -i & 0 \\ i & 0 & -i \\ 0 & i & 0 \end{bmatrix}$$

^spin-1-along-y

> [!cor] Eigenstates of $\mathbf{\hat{S}}_y$ for Spin-1
> The [[2.7 - Function Spaces#^eigenfunction]] of [[#^spin-1-along-y]] $\mathbf{\hat{S}}_y$ with eigenvalues $\hbar, 0, -\hbar$:
>
> $$\Ket{1}_y = \frac{1}{2}\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ i\sqrt{2} \\ -1 \end{bmatrix}, \quad \Ket{0}_y = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}, \quad \Ket{-1}_y = \frac{1}{2}\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ -i\sqrt{2} \\ -1 \end{bmatrix}$$

### Commutation Relations

> [!thm] Spin-1 Commutators
> The [[2.2 - Special Matrices#^commutator]] of the spin-1 operators satisfy the same angular momentum commutation relations as spin-1/2:
>
> $$[\mathbf{\hat{S}}_i, \mathbf{\hat{S}}_j] = i\hbar\epsilon_{ijk}\mathbf{\hat{S}}_k$$
>
>

^spin-1-commutators

> [!cor] Non-Simultaneous Measurements for Spin-1
> Since the spin operators are not [[2.2 - Special Matrices#^commuting-matrices]], by [[6.0 - Postulates#^commuting-operators-share-eigenstates]], different spin components cannot be measured simultaneously. This holds for all spin systems regardless of the spin quantum number.

### Expectation Values

> [!thm] Expectation Values for Spin-1
> For a spin-1 system in state $\Ket{\psi}$, the [[6.2 - Uncertainty#^expectation-value]] of spin measurements along any axis are bounded:
>
> $$-\hbar \leq \langle \mathbf{\hat{S}}_i \rangle \leq \hbar \quad \text{for } i \in \{x, y, z\}$$
>
> For a particle in eigenstate $\Ket{1}_z$:
> $$\langle \mathbf{\hat{S}}_z \rangle = \hbar, \quad \langle \mathbf{\hat{S}}_x \rangle = 0, \quad \langle \mathbf{\hat{S}}_y \rangle = 0$$
>
> For a particle in eigenstate $\Ket{0}_z$:
> $$\langle \mathbf{\hat{S}}_z \rangle = 0, \quad \langle \mathbf{\hat{S}}_x \rangle = 0, \quad \langle \mathbf{\hat{S}}_y \rangle = 0$$

^expectation-values-for-spin-1

### Uncertainty Relations

> [!thm] Uncertainty for Spin-1
> By the [[6.2 - Uncertainty#^uncertainty]] Uncertainty Principle, for any two orthogonal spin components:
>
> $$\Delta \mathbf{\hat{S}}_x \, \Delta \mathbf{\hat{S}}_y \geq \frac{1}{2}\left|\langle [\mathbf{\hat{S}}_x, \mathbf{\hat{S}}_y] \rangle\right| = \frac{\hbar}{2}\left|\langle \mathbf{\hat{S}}_z \rangle\right|$$
>
> For a particle in eigenstate $\Ket{1}_z$:
> $$\Delta \mathbf{\hat{S}}_z = 0, \quad \Delta \mathbf{\hat{S}}_x = \frac{\hbar}{\sqrt{2}}, \quad \Delta \mathbf{\hat{S}}_y = \frac{\hbar}{\sqrt{2}}$$
>
> and thus: $\Delta \mathbf{\hat{S}}_x \, \Delta \mathbf{\hat{S}}_y = \frac{\hbar^2}{2}$
>
> For a particle in eigenstate $\Ket{0}_z$:
> $$\Delta \mathbf{\hat{S}}_z = 0, \quad \Delta \mathbf{\hat{S}}_x = \frac{\hbar}{\sqrt{2}}, \quad \Delta \mathbf{\hat{S}}_y = \frac{\hbar}{\sqrt{2}}$$
>
> and thus: $\Delta \mathbf{\hat{S}}_x \, \Delta \mathbf{\hat{S}}_y = \frac{\hbar^2}{2}$ (saturating the uncertainty bound since $\langle \mathbf{\hat{S}}_z \rangle = 0$)

^uncertainty-for-spin-1
