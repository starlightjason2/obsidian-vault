# 6.10 - LCAO

> [!def] Delta Well Orbital
> The electron orbital of a molecule with $N$ atoms can be approximated as a potential $V(x)$ of $N$ [[6.5 - Potential Wells#^delta-function-well|Delta Wells]].
> $$V(x)=-\gamma \sum_{n=1}^{N}{\delta(x-na)}$$
>
>

^delta-well-orbital

> [!def] LCAO
> The Linear Combination of Atomic Orbitals approximates each molecular orbital as a superposition of atomic eigenstates $\Ket{u_{n}}$, modeling each atom as a [[6.5 - Potential Wells#^potential-well|Potential Well]] with spacing $a$. Two assumptions reduce the [[6.3 - Energy#^energy-basis|Energy Basis]] to a tridiagonal [[2.8 - Linear Operators#^matrix-representation-of-a-linear-operator|Matrix]]:
>
> 1. Non-adjacent [[6.3 - Energy#^hamiltonian-matrix-element|Matrix Elements]] vanish: $\bra{u_{n}}\hat{H}\ket{u_{n+2}}=0$.
> 2. Diagonals are the [[#^lcao-alpha|Onsite Integral]] $\alpha$, off-diagonals the [[#^0fnlhw|Hopping Integral]] $\beta$.
>
> The molecular wavefunction is the span:
> $$\psi(x)=\sum_{n=1}^{N}c_{n}u_{n}(x)$$

^8ffeec

> [!thm] LCAO Onsite Integral
> In an [[#^8ffeec]] approximation, the diagonal [[6.3 - Energy#^hamiltonian-matrix-element]] of an atom in its own well is its isolated energy $\bra{u_{n}} \hat{H} \ket{u_{n}}$, with an exponentially small correction from the neighbouring wells in the molecule:
> $$\alpha\equiv\bra{u_{n}}\hat{H}\ket{u_{n}}=\alpha\big(1+2e^{-4q_{0}a}\big)\approx\alpha$$

^lcao-alpha

`\begin{proof}`@[[#^lcao-alpha]]
Each atomic orbital $u_{n}(x)=\sqrt{q_{0}}\,e^{-q_{0}|x-na|}$ peaks at its own well at site $na$ and decays exponentially elsewhere. Split the molecular Hamiltonian into the single-well [[6.3 - Energy#^hamiltonian|Hamiltonian]]:

$$\hat{H}_{n}=\hat{p}^{2}/(2m)-\gamma\delta(x-na)$$

Plus the deltas of all other wells $V_{\text{other}}=-\gamma\sum_{m\neq n}\delta(x-ma)$:

$$\hat{H}=\hat{H}_{n}+V_{\text{other}},\qquad\hat{H}_{n}u_{n}=\alpha u_{n}$$

The single-well identity gives $\gamma q_{0}=-2\alpha$:

$$q_{0}=\frac{m\gamma}{\hbar^{2}}\implies\gamma q_{0}=\frac{m\gamma^{2}}{\hbar^{2}}=-2\alpha$$

By orthonormality $\braket{n|n}=1$. The [[3.5 - Step and Delta#^integrating-the-delta-function|Dirac Delta]] sifts $|u_{n}|^{2}$ at each other well's site $ma$, where $u_{n}$ is at the exponential tail $|u_{n}(ma)|^{2}=q_{0}e^{-2q_{0}|m-n|a}$. For the diatomic with one other well at separation $2a$, this is $q_{0}e^{-4q_{0}a}$:

$$
\begin{align}
\bra{u_{n}}\hat{H}\ket{u_{n}}&=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}u_{n}^{*}\big(\hat{H}_{n}+V_{\text{other}}\big)u_{n}\,dx \\[0.5em]
&=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}u_{n}^{*}\hat{H}_{n}u_{n}\,dx-\gamma\sum_{m\neq n}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}|u_{n}(x)|^{2}\,\delta(x-ma)\,dx \\[0.5em]
&=\alpha\!\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}|u_{n}(x)|^{2}\,dx-\gamma\sum_{m\neq n}|u_{n}(ma)|^{2} \\[0.5em]
&=\alpha\,\cancel{\braket{n|n}}-\gamma q_{0}\sum_{m\neq n}e^{-2q_{0}|m-n|a} \\[0.5em]
&=\alpha-\gamma q_{0}\,e^{-4q_{0}a} \\[0.5em]
&=\alpha+2\alpha\,e^{-4q_{0}a} \\[0.5em]
&=\alpha\big(1+2e^{-4q_{0}a}\big)\approx\alpha
\end{align}
$$
`\end{proof}`

> [!thm] LCAO Hopping Integral
> In an [[#^8ffeec]] approximation, the off-diagonal [[6.3 - Energy#^hamiltonian-matrix-element]] between neighboring atoms is the **hopping integral** $\beta$, encoding the strength of a covalent bond:
> $$\beta\equiv\bra{u_{n}}\hat{H}\ket{u_{n+1}}=\alpha(2q_{0}a+3)\,e^{-2q_{0}a}$$

^0fnlhw

`\begin{proof}`@[[#^0fnlhw]]
Take neighboring orbitals $u_{1}(x)=\sqrt{q_{0}}\,e^{-q_{0}|x-a|}$ and $u_{2}(x)=\sqrt{q_{0}}\,e^{-q_{0}|x+a|}$. The orbital overlap splits at $x=\pm a$:

$$
\begin{align}
\braket{1|2}&=q_{0}\!\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{-q_{0}|x-a|}e^{-q_{0}|x+a|}\,dx \\[0.5em]
&=q_{0}\!\left[\int_{-\infty}^{-a}e^{2q_{0}x}\,dx+\int_{-a}^{a}e^{-2q_{0}a}\,dx+\int_{a}^{\infty}e^{-2q_{0}x}\,dx\right] \\[0.5em]
&=q_{0}\!\left[\left(\frac{e^{2q_{0}x}}{2q_{0}}\right)\bigg|_{-\infty}^{-a}+\left(e^{-2q_{0}a}x\right)\bigg|_{-a}^{a}-\left(\frac{e^{-2q_{0}x}}{2q_{0}}\right)\bigg|_{a}^{\infty}\right] \\[0.5em]
&=(1+2q_{0}a)\,e^{-2q_{0}a}
\end{align}
$$

^overlap-12

Split $\hat{H}=\hat{H}_{1}-\gamma\delta(x+a)$ so the kinetic + nearest delta collapses on $u_{1}$ via $\hat{H}_{1}u_{1}=\alpha u_{1}$, and the residual $\delta(x+a)$ sifts $u_{2}^{*}u_{1}$ at $x=-a$ where $u_{2}^{*}(-a)u_{1}(-a)=q_{0}e^{-2q_{0}a}$:

$$
\begin{align}
\bra{2}\hat{H}\ket{1}&=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}u_{2}^{*}\big(\hat{H}_{1}-\gamma\delta(x+a)\big)u_{1}\,dx \\[0.5em]
&=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}u_{2}^{*}\hat{H}_{1}u_{1}\,dx-\gamma\!\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}u_{2}^{*}(x)\,\delta(x+a)\,u_{1}(x)\,dx \\[0.5em]
&=\alpha\!\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}u_{2}^{*}u_{1}\,dx-\gamma\,u_{2}^{*}(-a)\,u_{1}(-a) \\[0.5em]
&=\alpha\braket{2|1}-\gamma\,u_{2}^{*}(-a)\,u_{1}(-a) \\[0.5em]
&=\alpha(1+2q_{0}a)\,e^{-2q_{0}a}-\gamma q_{0}\,e^{-2q_{0}a}
\end{align}
$$

Pulling out the common $e^{-2q_{0}a}$ and applying the identity $\gamma q_{0}=-2\alpha$ flips the sign of the second term:

$$
\begin{align}
\beta&=e^{-2q_{0}a}\big[\alpha(1+2q_{0}a)-\gamma q_{0}\big] \\[0.5em]
&=e^{-2q_{0}a}\big[\alpha(1+2q_{0}a)+2\alpha\big] \\[0.5em]
&=\alpha\,e^{-2q_{0}a}\big(1+2q_{0}a+2\big) \\[0.5em]
&=\alpha(2q_{0}a+3)\,e^{-2q_{0}a}
\end{align}
$$
`\end{proof}`

## Diatomic Molecule

### Eigenstates

> [!thm] Diatomic LCAO Solution
> The [[#^8ffeec]] of a diatomic molecule ($N=2$) can be expressed as a $2\times2$ Hamiltonian built from the eigenstates $u_{1}(x)=\Braket{x | 1}, \ u_{2}(x)=\Braket{x | 2}$ of each atom.
> $$\hat{H}\doteq\begin{bmatrix}
 \bra{1}\hat{H} \ket{1} & \bra{1}\hat{H} \ket{2} \\
\bra{2}\hat{H} \ket{1} & \bra{2}\hat{H} \ket{2}
\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}
 \alpha& \beta \\
\beta & \alpha
\end{bmatrix}$$
>
> The energy eigenvalues are $E=\alpha\pm\beta$, and the eigenstates split into even and odd combinations of $u_{1}, u_{2}$.
> $$\psi(x)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \begin{bmatrix}
1 & 1 \\
1 & -1
\end{bmatrix}=\begin{cases}
\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(u_{1}(x)+u_{2}(x)), & \text{even} \\[0.5em]
\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(u_{1}(x)-u_{2}(x)) ,& \text{odd}
\end{cases}$$
>
>
>

^64a138

`\begin{proof}`@[[#^64a138]]
The diatomic potential $V(x)=-\gamma\delta(x-a)-\gamma\delta(x+a)$ is symmetric, so by [[6.3 - Energy#^symmetric-potential-parity|Symmetric Potentials Share Parity Eigenstates]] the $\{u_{1},u_{2}\}$ subspace splits into even and odd combinations. Solving the [[2.6 - Eigenvectors and Eigenvalues#^characteristic-polynomial|Characteristic Polynomial]] $|\hat{H}-\lambda I|=0$:

$$(\lambda-\alpha)^{2}=\beta^{2}\implies\lambda=\alpha\pm\beta$$

For $\lambda=\alpha+\beta$, the eigenvector satisfies $-\beta c_{1}+\beta c_{2}=0$, giving the symmetric $\ket{+}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\end{pmatrix}$. For $\lambda=\alpha-\beta$, $c_{1}=-c_{2}$ gives the antisymmetric $\ket{-}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\begin{pmatrix}1\\-1\end{pmatrix}$.
`\end{proof}`

> [!def] Bonding State
> For a [[#^64a138|Diatomic LCAO Solution]] with $\beta<0$, the **bonding state** $\ket{+}=\Braket{x | \psi_{+}}$ sits at energy $\alpha+\beta$, lower than either isolated atom's $\alpha$.
>
> $$\ket{+}=\tfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(\ket{1}+\ket{2}),\qquad E_{+}=\alpha+\beta$$
> The bonding state is formed by the symmetric combination so the wavefunction adds constructively in the region between atoms, where it collapses to a [[3.7.1 - Sinusoidals#^cosh|Hyperbolic Cosine]].
> $$\psi_{+}(x)=\sqrt{2q_{0}}\,e^{-q_{0}a}\cosh(q_{0}x),\qquad |x|<a$$

^bonding-state

> [!def] Antibonding State
> For the [[#^64a138|Diatomic LCAO Solution]] with hopping $\beta<0$, the **antibonding state** $\ket{-}=\Braket{x | \psi_{-}}$ sits at energy $\alpha-\beta$, raised above the isolated $\alpha$.
>
>
>
> $$\ket{-}=\tfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(\ket{1}-\ket{2}),\qquad E_{-}=\alpha-\beta$$
> The antibonding state is formed by the antisymmetric combination so the wavefunction has a node between the atoms and overlap is destructive, taking the form of a [[3.7.1 - Sinusoidals#^sinh|Hyperbolic Sine]] that vanishes at the midpoint.
> $$\psi_{-}(x)=\sqrt{2q_{0}}\,e^{-q_{0}a}\sinh(q_{0}x),\qquad |x|<a$$

^antibonding-state

### Delta Well Realization

> [!thm] Diatomic Delta LCAO
> The [[#^64a138|Diatomic LCAO Solution]] for [[#^delta-well-orbital|Delta Well Orbitals]] $u_{n}(x)=\sqrt{q_{0}}\,e^{-q_{0}|x-na|}$ where $q_{0}=m\gamma/\hbar^{2}$ is the following:
> $$\hat{H}=\begin{bmatrix}
 \alpha& \beta \\
\beta & \alpha
\end{bmatrix};\qquad\alpha=-\frac{m\gamma^{2}}{2\hbar^{2}}, \quad \beta=\alpha e^{{-2q_{0}a}}(2q_{0}a+3)$$
>
>
>

^dh6i6x

`\begin{proof}`@[[#^dh6i6x]]
Project $\Ket{\psi}=c_{1}u_{1}+c_{2}u_{2}$ onto basis $u_{1}(x)=\sqrt{q_{0}}\,e^{-q_{0}|x-a|}$, $u_{2}(x)=\sqrt{q_{0}}\,e^{-q_{0}|x+a|}$. The diagonal entries collapse to $\alpha$ by [[#^lcao-alpha|LCAO Onsite Integral]], and the off-diagonals collapse to $\beta=\alpha(2q_{0}a+3)e^{-2q_{0}a}$ by [[#^0fnlhw|LCAO Hopping Integral]]. Hermiticity makes $\bra{1}\hat{H}\ket{2}=\bra{2}\hat{H}\ket{1}=\beta$ real.
`\end{proof}`

## Triatomic Molecule

> [!example] Triatomic Chain
> Three identical atoms in an open chain at positions $-a, 0, +a$, modeled as [[#^8ffeec|LCAO]]. End atoms couple only to the central atom (no wraparound), giving a tridiagonal Hamiltonian:
> $$\hat{H}=\begin{bmatrix}\alpha & \beta & 0 \\ \beta & \alpha & \beta \\ 0 & \beta & \alpha\end{bmatrix}$$
>
> Solving the [[2.6 - Eigenvectors and Eigenvalues#^characteristic-polynomial|Characteristic Polynomial]] $\det(\hat{H}-\lambda I)=(\alpha-\lambda)\big[(\alpha-\lambda)^{2}-2\beta^{2}\big]=0$ gives three eigenvalues with eigenvectors:
> $$
> \begin{align}
> E_{0}&=\alpha,&\Ket{\psi_{0}}&=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\begin{pmatrix}-1\\0\\1\end{pmatrix} \\[1em]
> E_{\pm}&=\alpha\pm\sqrt{2}\beta,&\Ket{\psi_{\pm}}&=\frac{1}{2}\begin{pmatrix}1\\\pm\sqrt{2}\\1\end{pmatrix}
> \end{align}
> $$
>
> The three position-space eigenfunctions are linear combinations of single-well orbitals $u_{n}(x)=\sqrt{q_{0}}\,e^{-q_{0}|x-na|}$:
> $$
> \begin{align}
> \psi_{0}(x)&=\frac{\sqrt{q_{0}}}{\sqrt{2}}\big(-e^{-q_{0}|x+a|}+e^{-q_{0}|x-a|}\big) \\[0.5em]
> \psi_{\pm}(x)&=\frac{\sqrt{q_{0}}}{2}\big(e^{-q_{0}|x+a|}\pm\sqrt{2}\,e^{-q_{0}|x|}+e^{-q_{0}|x-a|}\big)
> \end{align}
> $$

^triatomic-chain

## Multi-atom Ring

> [!thm] Periodic LCAO
> Consider the [[#^8ffeec|LCAO]] of $N$ identical atoms within a ring, where $u_{n+N}(x)=u_{n}(x)$. The system is equivalent to [[4.8 - Coupled Oscillators#^lattice|Masses on a Lattice]], with an $N\times N$ Hamiltonian:
>
> $$\hat{H}=\alpha\mathbf{I}+\beta\big(\mathbf{S}^{\uparrow}+\mathbf{S}^{\downarrow}\big)=\begin{bmatrix}
\alpha & \beta & 0 & \cdots & 0 & \beta \\
\beta & \alpha & \beta & \cdots & 0 & 0 \\
0 & \beta & \alpha & \cdots & 0 & 0 \\
\vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots & \vdots \\
0 & 0 & 0 & \cdots & \alpha & \beta \\
\beta & 0 & 0 & \cdots & \beta & \alpha
\end{bmatrix}$$
>
> The eigenstates are labeled by wavenumber $k_{l}=2\pi l/(Na)$ for $-N/2<l\leq N/2$, and form a band over the [[4.8 - Coupled Oscillators#^brillouin-zone|Brillouin Zone]] with energy ranging from $\alpha+2\beta$ at $k=0$ to $\alpha-2\beta$ at $k=\pm\pi/a$:
>
> $$\psi_{l}(x)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{N}}\sum_{n=1}^{N}e^{ink_{l}a}\,u_{n}(x-na),\qquad E(k_{l})=\alpha+2\beta\cos(k_{l}a)$$

^sdoqfo

`\begin{proof}`@[[#^sdoqfo]]
The [[2.2 - Special Matrices#^shift-up-matrix|Cyclic Up-Shift]] and [[2.2 - Special Matrices#^shift-down-matrix|Cyclic Down-Shift]] share eigenvectors $\Ket{l}=\tfrac{1}{\sqrt{N}}\sum_{n=1}^{N}e^{ink_{l}a}\Ket{n}$ with eigenvalues $e^{\pm ik_{l}a}$. Acting $\hat{H}$ on $\Ket{l}$:

$$
\begin{align}
\hat{H}\Ket{l}&=\big[\alpha\mathbf{I}+\beta(\mathbf{S}^{\uparrow}+\mathbf{S}^{\downarrow})\big]\Ket{l} \\[0.5em]
&=\alpha\Ket{l}+\beta\big(e^{ik_{l}a}+e^{-ik_{l}a}\big)\Ket{l} \\[0.5em]
&=\big[\alpha+2\beta\cos(k_{l}a)\big]\Ket{l}
\end{align}
$$

The [[4.0 - ODEs#^periodic-boundary-conditions|Periodic Boundary Condition]] $u_{n+N}=u_{n}$ forces $e^{iNk_{l}a}=1$, quantizing $k_{l}=2\pi l/(Na)$ for integer $l$. Restricting $l$ to $-N/2<l\leq N/2$ keeps $k_{l}$ inside the [[4.8 - Coupled Oscillators#^brillouin-zone|Brillouin Zone]] and exhausts all $N$ distinct eigenstates. The position-space wavefunction follows from $\braket{x|l}=\tfrac{1}{\sqrt{N}}\sum_{n}e^{ink_{l}a}\,u_{n}(x-na)$.
`\end{proof}`

> [!thm] Bloch's Theorem
> Every [[6.0 - Postulates#^eigenstate-postulate|Energy Eigenstate]] of the [[#^sdoqfo|Multi-atom LCAO Hamiltonian]] picks up a phase $e^{imka}$ under translation by $m$ [[4.8 - Coupled Oscillators#^lattice|Lattice Sites]].
> $$\psi_{k}(x+ma)=e^{imka}\,\psi_{k}(x)$$

^f19noz

`\begin{proof}`@[[#^f19noz]]
Shifting the [[#^sdoqfo|Multi-atom LCAO]] eigenstate $\psi_{k}(x)=\tfrac{1}{\sqrt{N}}\sum_{n}e^{ink a}u(x-na)$ by $ma$ and relabeling $n'=n-m$:

$$
\begin{align}
\psi_{k}(x+ma)&=\frac{1}{\sqrt{N}}\sum_{n}e^{inka}u(x-(n-m)a) \\[0.5em]
&=e^{imka}\frac{1}{\sqrt{N}}\sum_{n'}e^{in'ka}u(x-n'a) \\[0.5em]
&=e^{imka}\,\psi_{k}(x)
\end{align}
$$
`\end{proof}`

### Benzene Example

> [!example] Benzene Ring
> A ring of $N=6$ atoms modeled as a [[#^sdoqfo|Multi-atom LCAO Hamiltonian]]. Each atom contributes one orbital $\Ket{n}$ with onsite energy $\bra{n}\hat{H}\ket{n}=\alpha$, coupled only to its two ring neighbors with hopping $\bra{n}\hat{H}\ket{m}=\beta<0$ for $m=n\pm 1\pmod{6}$. All other matrix elements vanish.
>
> $$\hat{H}=\alpha\mathbf{I}+\beta\big(\mathbf{S}^{\uparrow}+\mathbf{S}^{\downarrow}\big)=\begin{bmatrix}
\alpha & \beta & 0 & 0 & 0 & \beta \\
\beta & \alpha & \beta & 0 & 0 & 0 \\
0 & \beta & \alpha & \beta & 0 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & \beta & \alpha & \beta & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 0 & \beta & \alpha & \beta \\
\beta & 0 & 0 & 0 & \beta & \alpha
\end{bmatrix}$$
>
> The [[2.2 - Special Matrices#^spectrum-of-cyclic-shifts|Cyclic Shift Eigenvectors]] indexed by $l\in\{0,\pm 1,\pm 2,3\}$ diagonalize $\hat{H}$ with components $\braket{n|l}=e^{i\pi nl/3}/\sqrt{6}$ and energies:
> $$\Ket{l}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{6}}\sum_{n=1}^{6}e^{i\pi nl/3}\Ket{n},\qquad E_{l}=\alpha+2\beta\cos\!\left(\frac{\pi l}{3}\right)$$
>
> The six modes collapse onto four distinct levels, with $E_{\pm 1}$ and $E_{\pm 2}$ each doubly degenerate by the symmetry $E_{l}=E_{-l}$. The unpaired endpoints generalize the diatomic [[#^bonding-state|Bonding]] and [[#^antibonding-state|Antibonding]] states across all six atoms:
> $$\begin{cases}
E_{0}=\alpha+2\beta, & \text{nondegenerate, fully bonding} \\[0.5em]
E_{\pm 1}=\alpha+\beta, & \text{doubly degenerate} \\[0.5em]
E_{\pm 2}=\alpha-\beta, & \text{doubly degenerate} \\[0.5em]
E_{3}=\alpha-2\beta, & \text{nondegenerate, fully antibonding}
\end{cases}$$

^benzene-ring

`\begin{proof}`@[[#^benzene-ring]]
The [[2.2 - Special Matrices#^shift-up-matrix|Cyclic Up-Shift]] acts on the proposed eigenvector as $\mathbf{S}^{\uparrow}\Ket{l}=e^{i\pi l/3}\Ket{l}$, and the [[2.2 - Special Matrices#^shift-down-matrix|Cyclic Down-Shift]] as $\mathbf{S}^{\downarrow}\Ket{l}=e^{-i\pi l/3}\Ket{l}$. Acting $\hat{H}$ on $\Ket{l}$:

$$
\begin{align}
\hat{H}\Ket{l}&=\big[\alpha\mathbf{I}+\beta(\mathbf{S}^{\uparrow}+\mathbf{S}^{\downarrow})\big]\Ket{l} \\[0.5em]
&=\alpha\Ket{l}+\beta\big(e^{i\pi l/3}+e^{-i\pi l/3}\big)\Ket{l} \\[0.5em]
&=\big[\alpha+2\beta\cos(\pi l/3)\big]\Ket{l}
\end{align}
$$

The label $l$ runs over a complete residue system mod $6$. Choosing $l\in\{-2,-1,0,1,2,3\}$ places the wavenumbers $k_{l}=\pi l/(3a)$ inside the [[4.8 - Coupled Oscillators#^brillouin-zone|Brillouin Zone]], and the six values exhaust all distinct eigenstates. Since [[3.7.1 - Sinusoidals#^d2a1f2|Cosine]] is even, $E_{l}=E_{-l}$, so $l=\pm 1$ share the level $\alpha+\beta$ and $l=\pm 2$ share $\alpha-\beta$. The endpoints $l=0$ and $l=3$ have no partner, giving the bonding ground state $\alpha+2\beta$ (where $\beta<0$ lowers the energy) and the antibonding top state $\alpha-2\beta$.
`\end{proof}`
