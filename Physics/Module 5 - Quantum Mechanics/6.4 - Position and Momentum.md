# 6.4 - Position and Momentum

> [!axm] Wave Particle Duality
> A free [[6.5 - Free Particles#^free-matter-wave|Particle]] behaves like a wave, with [[#^aace22|Momentum Eigenvalue]] $p$ quantized by the de Broglie wavenumber $k$ and [[6.3 - Energy#^energy-basis|Energy Eigenvalue]] $E$ quantized by angular frequency $\omega$.
> $$p=\hbar k,\qquad E=\hbar \omega$$

^wave-particle-duality

> [!thm] Position–Momentum Duality
> The [[3.7 - Periodic Functions#^fourier-transform|Fourier Transform]] performs a change of basis between the [[#^position-basis|Position Basis]] and the [[#^momentum-basis|Momentum Basis]] with the [[2.7 - Function Spaces#^completeness-function-relation]].
>
> $$\braket{x|p}=\frac{e^{ipx/\hbar}}{\sqrt{2\pi\hbar}},\qquad\braket{p|x}=\frac{e^{-ipx/\hbar}}{\sqrt{2\pi\hbar}}$$
>
> $$\psi(x)=\braket{x|\psi}=\int\braket{x|p}\braket{p|\psi}\,dp=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi\hbar}}\int e^{ipx/\hbar}\,\tilde{\psi}(p)\,dp\quad\text{(inverse FT)}$$
>
> $$\tilde{\psi}(p)=\braket{p|\psi}=\int\braket{p|x}\braket{x|\psi}\,dx=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi\hbar}}\int e^{-ipx/\hbar}\,\psi(x)\,dx\quad\text{(FT)}$$

^position-momentum-duality

## Position Space

> [!def] Position Observable
> Position is the [[6.0 - Postulates#^continuous-observable]] $\hat{x}$. In the [[#^position-basis|Position]] and [[#^momentum-basis|Momentum]] bases, $\hat{x}$ is expressed as:
>
> $$
> \begin{align}
> \hat{x}&\equiv x \tag{Position basis}\\[0.5em]
> \hat{x}&\equiv i\hbar\frac{\partial}{\partial p}\tag{Momentum basis}
> \end{align}
> $$
>
> The measured position eigenvalues $x$ obey the eigenvalue equation:
> $$\hat{x}\,\psi(x)=x\,\psi(x)$$
>
> For the [[6.6 - Quantum Harmonic Oscillator#^qho-hamiltonian|Quantum Harmonic Oscillator]], $\hat{x}$ also factors into [[6.6 - Quantum Harmonic Oscillator#^x-p-from-ladder|Ladder Operators]].

^f3f4cf

> [!def] Position Basis
> The eigenbasis of the [[#^f3f4cf|Position Observable]] $\hat{x}$ consists of position eigenstates $\ket{x}$ satisfying $\hat{x}\ket{x}=x\ket{x}$.
>
> 1. $$\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\ket{x}\bra{x}\,dx=\hat{I}$$
> 2. $$\braket{x_1|x_2}=\delta(x_1-x_2)$$

^position-basis

### Eigenstates

> [!def] Position Eigenstate
> The [[6.0 - Postulates#^eigenstate-postulate|Eigenstate]] $\ket{x_0}$ of the [[#^f3f4cf]] expressed in the [[#^position-basis|Position Basis]] and [[#^momentum-basis|Momentum Basis]]:
>
> $$
> \begin{align}
> \braket{x|x_{0}}&=\delta(x-x_{0}) \tag{Position basis}\\[0.5em]
> \braket{p|x_{0}}&=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi\hbar}}\,e^{-ix_{0}p/\hbar}\tag{Momentum basis}
> \end{align}
> $$

^xeigen

`\begin{proof}`@[[#^xeigen]]
The position eigenvalue equation in position space is
$$x\,\braket{x|x_0}=x_0\braket{x|x_0}$$
so $(x-x_0)\braket{x|x_0}=0$, which is satisfied only by $\braket{x|x_0}=\delta(x-x_0)$.

For the momentum basis representation, insert a complete set of position states using the [[#^position-basis|Position Basis]] completeness relation and apply the [[6.4 - Position and Momentum#^positionmomentum-duality|Position-Momentum Duality]]:
$$\braket{p|x_0}=\int\braket{p|x}\braket{x|x_0}\,dx=\int\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi\hbar}}\,e^{-ipx/\hbar}\,\delta(x-x_0)\,dx=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi\hbar}}\,e^{-ipx_0/\hbar}$$
`\end{proof}`

### Expectation Value

> [!def] Position Expectation Value
> The [[6.2 - Uncertainty#^expectation-value|Expectation Value]] $\langle\hat{x}\rangle$ of [[#^f3f4cf|Position]] is complementary in the two bases:
>
> $$
> \begin{align}
> \langle\hat{x}\rangle &= \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} x\,|\psi(x)|^2\,dx \tag{Position basis} \\[1em]
> \langle\hat{x}\rangle &= \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \tilde{\psi}^*(p)\,i\hbar\frac{\partial\tilde{\psi}}{\partial p}\,dp \tag{Momentum basis}
> \end{align}
> $$

^xexpval

`\begin{proof}`@[[#^xexpval]]
Insert the [[#^position-basis|Position Basis]] resolution of the identity into $\langle\hat{x}\rangle=\braket{\psi|\hat{x}|\psi}$ and use $\hat{x}\ket{x}=x\ket{x}$:

$$\langle\hat{x}\rangle=\int\braket{\psi|x}\,x\,\braket{x|\psi}\,dx=\int x\,|\psi(x)|^{2}\,dx$$

In the momentum basis, $\hat{x}$ acts as $i\hbar\,\partial_{p}$. Therefore:

$$\langle\hat{x}\rangle=\int\tilde{\psi}^{*}(p)\,i\hbar\frac{\partial\tilde{\psi}}{\partial p}\,dp$$

`\end{proof}`

### Uncertainty

> [!def] Position Uncertainty
> The [[6.2 - Uncertainty#^uncertainty|Uncertainty]] $\Delta\hat{x}$ of [[#^f3f4cf|Position]] is the root-mean-square spread about the [[#^xexpval]] $\langle\hat{x}\rangle$:
> $$\Delta\hat{x}=\sqrt{\langle\hat{x}^{2}\rangle-\langle\hat{x}\rangle^{2}}$$
> The second moment is complementary in the two bases:
>
> $$
> \begin{align}
> \langle\hat{x}^{2}\rangle &= \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} x^{2}\,|\psi(x)|^{2}\,dx \tag{Position basis}\\[1em]
> \langle\hat{x}^{2}\rangle &= \hbar^{2}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\left|\frac{\partial\tilde{\psi}}{\partial p}\right|^{2}dp \tag{Momentum basis}
> \end{align}
> $$

^xuncertainty

`\begin{proof}`@[[#^xuncertainty]]
The position-basis form repeats the [[#^xexpval]] argument with $\hat{x}^{2}\ket{x}=x^{2}\ket{x}$,

$$\langle\hat{x}^{2}\rangle=\int\braket{\psi|x}\,x^{2}\,\braket{x|\psi}\,dx=\int x^{2}\,|\psi(x)|^{2}\,dx$$

In the momentum basis $\hat{x}^{2}$ acts as $-\hbar^{2}\,\partial_{p}^{2}$. Integrating by parts and discarding boundary terms (since $\tilde{\psi}\to 0$ as $|p|\to\infty$),

$$\langle\hat{x}^{2}\rangle=-\hbar^{2}\int\tilde{\psi}^{*}\,\frac{\partial^{2}\tilde{\psi}}{\partial p^{2}}\,dp=\hbar^{2}\int\left|\frac{\partial\tilde{\psi}}{\partial p}\right|^{2}dp$$
`\end{proof}`

## Momentum Space

> [!def] Momentum Observable
> Momentum is the [[6.0 - Postulates#^continuous-observable]] $\hat{p}$. In the [[#^position-basis |Position]] and [[#^momentum-basis]], $\hat{p}$ is expressed as:
>
> $$
> \begin{align}
> \hat{p}&\equiv p \tag{Momentum basis}\\[0.5em]
> \hat{p}&\equiv-i\hbar \frac{d}{dx}\tag{Position basis}
> \end{align}
> $$
>
> The measured momentum eigenvalues $p=\hbar k$ obey [[#^wave-particle-duality]], with the eigenvalue equation:
> $$\hat{p}\,\psi(x)=\hbar k\,\psi(x)$$
>
> For the [[6.6 - Quantum Harmonic Oscillator#^qho-hamiltonian|Quantum Harmonic Oscillator]], $\hat{p}$ also factors into [[6.6 - Quantum Harmonic Oscillator#^x-p-from-ladder|Ladder Operators]].

^aace22

> [!def] Momentum Basis
> The eigenbasis of the [[#^aace22|Momentum Observable]] $\hat{p}$ consists of [[#^peigen|Momentum Eigenstates]] $\ket{p}$ satisfying the eigenvalue equation $\hat{p}\ket{p}=p\ket{p}$. As a complete orthonormal basis:
>
> 1. $$\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\ket{p}\bra{p}\,dp=\hat{I}$$
> 2. $$\braket{p_1|p_2}=\delta(p_1-p_2)$$

^momentum-basis

### Eigenstates

> [!def] Momentum Eigenstate
> The [[6.0 - Postulates#^eigenstate-postulate|Eigenstate]] $\ket{p_0}$ of the [[#^aace22]] expressed in the [[#^position-basis|Position Basis]] and [[#^momentum-basis|Momentum Basis]]:
>
> $$
> \begin{align}
> \braket{x|p_{0}}&=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi\hbar}}\,e^{ip_{0}x/\hbar} \tag{Position basis}\\[0.5em]
> \braket{p|p_{0}}&=\delta(p-p_{0})\tag{Momentum basis}
> \end{align}
> $$

^peigen

`\begin{proof}`@[[#^peigen]]
The momentum eigenvalue equation in position space is
$$-i\hbar\,\frac{\partial\psi_p}{\partial x}=p\,\psi_p$$
Use the [[4.1.1 - First Order Linear ODEs#^homogeneous-solution-of-a-first-order-linear-ode]]: $\psi_p(x)=Ce^{ipx/\hbar}$. The normalization constant $C=1/\sqrt{2\pi\hbar}$ follows from $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\left\lvert{\psi_{p}}\right\rvert^{2}\,dx=1$.

Furthermore, with the completeness relation and [[3.7 - Periodic Functions#^fourier-transform-of-dirac-delta]], we see:
$$\int\braket{x_1|p}\braket{p|x_2}\,dp=\delta(x_1-x_2)$$

`\end{proof}`

### Expectation Value

> [!def] Momentum Expectation Value
> The [[6.2 - Uncertainty#^expectation-value|Expectation Value]] $\langle\hat{p}\rangle$ of [[#^aace22|Momentum]] is complementary in the two bases:
>
> $$
> \begin{align}
> \langle\hat{p}\rangle &= \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} p\,|\tilde{\psi}(p)|^2\,dp \tag{Momentum basis} \\[0.5em]
> \langle\hat{p}\rangle &= \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \psi^*(x)\left(-i\hbar\frac{\partial\psi}{\partial x}\right)dx \tag{Position basis}
> \end{align}
> $$

^pexpval

`\begin{proof}`@[[#^pexpval]]
The momentum-basis form mirrors the position-basis derivation in [[#^xexpval]]: insert the [[#^momentum-basis]] resolution of the identity into $\langle\hat{p}\rangle=\braket{\psi|\hat{p}|\psi}$ and use $\hat{p}\ket{p}=p\ket{p}$ to get

$$\langle\hat{p}\rangle=\int p\,|\tilde{\psi}(p)|^{2}\,dp$$

In the position basis, $\hat{p}$ acts as $-i\hbar\,\partial_{x}$, so

$$\langle\hat{p}\rangle=\int\psi^{*}(x)\left(-i\hbar\frac{\partial\psi}{\partial x}\right)dx$$

`\end{proof}`

### Uncertainty

> [!def] Momentum Uncertainty
> The [[6.2 - Uncertainty#^uncertainty|Uncertainty]] $\Delta\hat{p}$ of [[#^aace22|Momentum]] is the root-mean-square spread about the [[#^pexpval]] $\langle\hat{p}\rangle$:
> $$\Delta\hat{p}=\sqrt{\langle\hat{p}^{2}\rangle-\langle\hat{p}\rangle^{2}}$$
> The second moment $\langle \hat{p}^{2} \rangle$ is complementary in the two bases:
>
> $$
> \begin{align}
> \langle\hat{p}^{2}\rangle &= \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} p^{2}\,|\tilde{\psi}(p)|^{2}\,dp \tag{Momentum basis}\\[1em]
> \langle\hat{p}^{2}\rangle &= \hbar^{2}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\left|\frac{\partial\psi}{\partial x}\right|^{2}dx \tag{Position basis}
> \end{align}
> $$

^puncertainty

`\begin{proof}`@[[#^puncertainty]]
The momentum-basis form repeats the [[#^pexpval]] argument with $\hat{p}^{2}\ket{p}=p^{2}\ket{p}$,

$$\langle\hat{p}^{2}\rangle=\int p^{2}\,|\tilde{\psi}(p)|^{2}\,dp$$

In the position basis $\hat{p}^{2}$ acts as $-\hbar^{2}\,\partial_{x}^{2}$. Integrating by parts and discarding boundary terms (since $\psi\to 0$ as $|x|\to\infty$),

$$\langle\hat{p}^{2}\rangle=-\hbar^{2}\int\psi^{*}\,\frac{\partial^{2}\psi}{\partial x^{2}}\,dx=\hbar^{2}\int\left|\frac{\partial\psi}{\partial x}\right|^{2}dx$$
`\end{proof}`
