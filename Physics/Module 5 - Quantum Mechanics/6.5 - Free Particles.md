# 6.5 - Free Particles

> [!def] Free Matter Wave
> A free quantum particle is described by a [[4.4 - Wave Equation#^plane-wave]] whose [[4.4 - Wave Equation#^dispersion-relation]] is given by [[6.4 - Position and Momentum#^wave-particle-duality]]:
> $$E=\frac{p^2}{2m}\implies \omega=\frac{\hbar k^2}{2m}$$
> $$\psi(x,t)=Ae^{i(kx-\omega t)}+Be^{-i(kx-\omega t)}$$

^free-matter-wave

`\begin{proof}`@[[#^free-matter-wave]]
Solve the [[6.3 - Energy#^time-invariant-schrdinger-equation]]. Let initial state $\psi(x,0)=\sum c_{n}\,\phi(x)$ be in the energy eigenbasis. The energy eigenvalue equation for zero potential is:
 $$\phi''(x) +\left( \frac{2mE}{\hbar^{2}} \right) \phi(x) = 0$$

This is a [[4.1 - Simple Harmonic Oscillation#^d9cde8]] where
$$k^2=\frac{2mE}{\hbar^2}$$
and has known solutions. Multiplying by $e^{-i\omega t}$ where $E=\hbar\omega$ gives the time-dependent general solution.
`\end{proof}`

## Wave Packets

> [!def] Quantum Wave Packet
> A [[4.7 - Wave Packets#^wave-packet|Wave Packet]] formed from a superposition of [[6.5 - Free Particles#^free-matter-wave|Free Matter Waves]]. The weight $g(k)=\tilde\psi(k)$ is the momentum-space wavefunction, obtained from the initial position-space state via [[6.4 - Position and Momentum#^positionmomentum-duality|Position-Momentum Duality]]:
> $$\psi(x,t)=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\tilde\psi(k)\,e^{i(kx-\omega t)}\,dk$$
> Matter waves are dispersive under $\hbar\omega=\hbar^2k^2/2m$, so $v_g\neq v_\phi$ and the packet spreads over time. Notice $v_{g}$ is equivalent to velocity from classical [[1.6 - Linear Momentum and Collisions#^momentum]].
> $$\begin{align}
> v_\phi&=\frac{\omega}{k}=\frac{p}{2m} \\[0.5em]
> v_g&=\frac{d\omega}{dk}=\frac{p}{m}
> \end{align}$$

^quantum-wave-packet

### Gaussian Wave Packet

> [!exm] Gaussian Wave Packet
> A Gaussian [[#^quantum-wave-packet|Quantum Wave Packet]] of initial width $\sigma$ centered at $k_0$. The wavefunction $\psi(x)$ and $\tilde{\psi}(k)$ in position and momentum space, respectively:
> $$\begin{align}
\psi(x)&=\left(\frac{1}{\sigma^{2}\pi}\right)^{1/4}e^{-x^{2}/2\sigma^{2}} \\[0.5em] \tilde{\psi}(k)&=\left(\frac{\sigma^{2}}{\pi}\right)^{1/4}e^{-\sigma^{2}k^{2}/2}
\end{align}$$
> The Fourier transform gives a Gaussian in momentum space with width $1/\sigma$, consistent with the [[6.2 - Uncertainty|Uncertainty Principle]]. Under time evolution the packet spreads, moving at [[4.7 - Wave Packets#^group-velocity|Group Velocity]] $v_g=\hbar k_0/m$ while its width $\sigma(t)$ grows.
> $$\begin{align}
> |\psi(x,t)|^2&=\frac{1}{\sigma(t)\sqrt{\pi}}\,\exp\!\left(-\frac{(x-v_g t)^2}{\sigma(t)^2}\right), \qquad
> \sigma(t)=\sigma\sqrt{1+\left(\frac{\hbar t}{m\sigma^2}\right)^{\!2}}
> \end{align}$$
> 

^gauss-wp

`\begin{proof}`@[[#^gauss-wp]]
For the momentum-space form: starting from $\tilde{\psi}(k)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}\int N\,e^{-x^{2}/2\sigma^{2}}e^{-ikx}\,dx$, combine exponents and complete the square:
$$\frac{x^{2}}{2\sigma^{2}}+ikx=\frac{1}{2\sigma^{2}}\!\left(x+ik\sigma^{2}\right)^{2}+\frac{k^{2}\sigma^{2}}{2}$$
Shifting $x\to x-ik\sigma^{2}$ leaves the [[3.9 - Integrals#^riemann-sum|Gaussian Integral]] unchanged by a contour argument, giving $\tilde{\psi}(k)=N\sigma\,e^{-k^{2}\sigma^{2}/2}$. Normalization yields $N=(\sigma^{2}\pi)^{-1/4}$.

For the time evolution: insert $g(k)=(\sigma^2/\pi)^{1/4}e^{-\sigma^2(k-k_0)^2/2}$ into $\psi(x,t)=\int g(k)e^{i(kx-\omega t)}\,dk$ with $\omega=\hbar k^2/2m$. Setting $\beta=\hbar/2m$ and completing the square in $k$:
$$-\frac{\sigma^2}{2}(k-k_0)^2+ikx-i\beta k^2 t=-\left(\frac{\sigma^2}{2}+i\beta t\right)(k-k_0)^2+i(k_0 x-\omega_0 t)+\cdots$$
With $a=\sigma^2/2+i\beta t$, the Gaussian integral gives $\sqrt{\pi/a}$, so $\psi(x,t)\propto e^{i(k_0x-\omega_0t)}\,e^{-(x-v_g t)^2/4a}$. Taking $|\psi|^2$ eliminates the phase; the real part of $1/4a$ gives $\sigma(t)$.
`\end{proof}`

### Rectangular Packet

> [!exm] Fourier Transform of a Rectangular Packet
> A rectangular wave packet $\psi(x)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{a}}$ for $|x|\leq a/2$, zero otherwise, has the [[3.8 - Fourier Transform#^fourier-transform|Fourier Transform]]:
> $$\tilde{\psi}(k)=\sqrt{\frac{a}{2\pi}}\,\operatorname{sinc}\!\left(\frac{(k-k_0)a}{2}\right)$$
> The momentum distribution is a sinc function centred at $k_0$, with first zeros at $\Delta k=2\pi/a$. A wider packet gives a narrower momentum spread.
