# 6.6 - Quantum Harmonic Oscillator

> [!def] Quantum Harmonic Oscillator
> Models the vibration of nuclei in molecules with a quadratic potential $V(x)=\frac{1}{2}m\omega^{2}\hat{x}^{2}$. The [[4.1 - Simple Harmonic Oscillation#^sho-max-energy]] energy carries directly over to the [[6.3 - Energy#^hamiltonian|Hamiltonian]]:
> $$\hat{H}=\frac{\hat{p}^{2}}{2m}+\frac{1}{2}m\omega^{2}\hat{x}^{2}$$
>
> Or, in terms of the [[#^number-operator|Number Operator]] $\hat{N}$:
> $$\hat{H}=\hbar\omega\!\left(\hat{a}^{\dagger}\hat{a}+\frac{1}{2}\right)=\hbar\omega\!\left(\hat{N}+\frac{1}{2}\right)$$

^qho-hamiltonian

## Ladder Operators

> [!def] Number Operator
> The number operator $\hat{N}=\hat{a}^{\dagger}\hat{a}$ is a [[6.0 - Postulates#^discrete-observable|Discrete Observable]] built from the [[#^creation-operator|Creation]] and [[#^annihilation-operator|Annihilation]] operators, with [[2.6 - Eigenvectors and Eigenvalues#^eiegenvalue|Eigenvalues]] $n\in\mathbb{Z}^{+}$ counting excitation quanta in the eigenstate $\ket{n}$.
> $$\hat{N}\equiv\hat{a}^{\dagger}\hat{a},\qquad \hat{N}\ket{n}=n\ket{n}$$

^number-operator

> [!thm] Position and Momentum from Ladder Operators
> Inverting the [[#^annihilation-operator|Annihilation]] and [[#^creation-operator|Creation]] operators expresses $\hat{x}$ and $\hat{p}$ as combinations of $\hat{a}$ and $\hat{a}^{\dagger}$.
> $$\hat{x}=\sqrt{\frac{\hbar}{2m\omega}}\!\left(\hat{a}+\hat{a}^{\dagger}\right),\qquad \hat{p}=\frac{1}{i}\sqrt{\frac{m\omega\hbar}{2}}\!\left(\hat{a}-\hat{a}^{\dagger}\right)$$

^x-p-from-ladder

### Annihilation

> [!def] Annihilation Operator
> The **annihilation operator** $\hat{a}$ is the Hermitian conjugate of the [[#^creation-operator]]. It's a non-Hermitian combination of the [[6.4 - Position and Momentum#^f3f4cf|Position]] $\hat{x}$ and [[6.4 - Position and Momentum#^aace22|Momentum]] $\hat{p}$ that lowers an eigenstate of the [[#^qho-hamiltonian]] by one quantum.
>
> $$\hat{a}=\sqrt{\frac{m\omega}{2\hbar}}\!\left(\hat{x}+\frac{i\hat{p}}{m\omega}\right)$$

^annihilation-operator

### Creation

> [!def] Creation Operator
> The **creation operator** $\hat{a}^{\dagger}$ is the Hermitian conjugate of the [[#^annihilation-operator|Annihilation Operator]] $\hat{a}$. It's a non-Hermitian combination of the [[6.4 - Position and Momentum#^f3f4cf|Position]] $\hat{x}$ and [[6.4 - Position and Momentum#^aace22|Momentum]] $\hat{p}$ that raises an eigenstate of the [[#^qho-hamiltonian]] by one quantum.
>
> $$\hat{a}^{\dagger}=\sqrt{\frac{m\omega}{2\hbar}}\!\left(\hat{x}-\frac{i\hat{p}}{m\omega}\right)$$

^creation-operator

## Classical Limit

> [!cor] Classical Limit of the Quantum Harmonic Oscillator
> Applying the [[6.2 - Uncertainty#^ehrenfest-theorem|Ehrenfest Theorem]] to the [[#^qho-hamiltonian|Quantum Harmonic Oscillator]] gives the [[4.1 - Simple Harmonic Oscillation#^simple-harmonic-oscillator-equation|Simple Harmonic Oscillator Equation]] for $\langle\hat{x}\rangle$.
> $$\frac{d^{2}\langle\hat{x}\rangle}{dt^{2}}+\omega^{2}\langle\hat{x}\rangle=0\implies \langle\hat{x}\rangle(t)=A\cos\omega t+B\sin\omega t$$

^qho-classical-limit

`\begin{proof}`@[[#^qho-classical-limit]]
By the [[6.2 - Uncertainty#^expectation-value-equation-of-motion|Expectation Value Equation of Motion]]:

$$\frac{d}{dt}\langle\hat{x}\rangle=\frac{1}{i\hbar}\langle[\hat{x},\hat{H}]\rangle=\frac{\langle\hat{p}\rangle}{m}$$

For $\langle\hat{p}\rangle$, $\hat{p}$ commutes with $\hat{p}^{2}$, so only the potential term contributes:

$$\frac{d}{dt}\langle\hat{p}\rangle=\frac{1}{i\hbar}\langle[\hat{p},\tfrac{1}{2}m\omega^{2}\hat{x}^{2}]\rangle=-m\omega^{2}\langle\hat{x}\rangle$$

Combining the two:

$$\frac{d^{2}\langle\hat{x}\rangle}{dt^{2}}=\frac{1}{m}\frac{d\langle\hat{p}\rangle}{dt}=-\omega^{2}\langle\hat{x}\rangle$$
`\end{proof}`
