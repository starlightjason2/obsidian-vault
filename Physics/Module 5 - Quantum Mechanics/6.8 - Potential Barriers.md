# 6.8 - Potential Barriers

## Step Potential

> [!def] Step Potential
> A piecewise constant potential with a single discontinuity at $x=0$:
> $$V(x)=\begin{cases}0 & x<0\\V_0 & x\geq 0\end{cases}$$
> Solve the [[6.3 - Energy#^ecd6eb|Time-Invariant Schrödinger Equation]] in each region, then match $\psi$ and $\psi'$ at $x=0$. With a particle incident from the left, there is no leftward-travelling wave for $x>0$.

^step

> [!exm] Step: $E > V_0$
> Define wavenumbers $k=\sqrt{2mE}/\hbar$ and $k'=\sqrt{2m(E-V_0)}/\hbar$. The eigenvalue equation is a [[4.1 - Simple Harmonic Oscillation#^d9cde8|Simple Harmonic ODE]] in each region, with general solution:
> $$\psi(x)=\begin{cases}e^{ikx}+Re^{-ikx} & x<0\\Te^{ik'x} & x\geq 0\end{cases}$$
> Continuity of $\psi$ and $\psi'$ at $x=0$ gives the reflection and transmission amplitudes:
> $$R=\frac{k-k'}{k+k'}, \qquad T=\frac{2k}{k+k'}$$
> The [[6.9 - Particle Flux#^by8z3w|Probability Current]] gives flux-weighted reflection and transmission coefficients:
> $$\mathcal{R}=|R|^2=\left(\frac{k-k'}{k+k'}\right)^2, \qquad \mathcal{T}=\frac{k'}{k}|T|^2=\frac{4kk'}{(k+k')^2}, \qquad \mathcal{R}+\mathcal{T}=1$$

^step-above

`\begin{proof}`@[[#^step-above]]
Continuity of $\psi$ at $x=0$: $1+R=T$. Continuity of $\psi'$ at $x=0$: $ik(1-R)=ik'T$. Substituting the first into the second:
$$k(1-R)=k'(1+R)\implies k-k'=R(k+k')\implies R=\frac{k-k'}{k+k'}$$
and then $T=1+R=2k/(k+k')$.

The incident, reflected, and transmitted [[6.9 - Particle Flux#^by8z3w|Probability Currents]] are $S_\text{inc}=\hbar k/m$, $S_\text{ref}=\hbar k|R|^2/m$, and $S_\text{trans}=\hbar k'|T|^2/m$. Dividing by $S_\text{inc}$ and using [[6.9 - Particle Flux#^c10pe6|Conservation]] $S_\text{inc}=S_\text{ref}+S_\text{trans}$ gives $\mathcal{R}+\mathcal{T}=1$.
`\end{proof}`

> [!exm] Step: $E < V_0$ (Evanescent)
> For $E<V_0$, define $\gamma=\sqrt{2m(V_0-E)}/\hbar$ so that $k'=i\gamma$ is imaginary. The eigenvalue equation for $x>0$ is a [[4.1.3 - Constant Coefficient ODEs#^382f29|Constant Coefficient ODE]] with real roots, and the normalisable solution decays exponentially:
> $$\psi(x)=\begin{cases}e^{ikx}+Re^{-ikx} & x<0\\Te^{-\gamma x} & x\geq 0\end{cases}$$
> Substituting $k'=i\gamma$ into the step result gives total reflection:
> $$R=\frac{k-i\gamma}{k+i\gamma}, \qquad |R|^2=\frac{k^2+\gamma^2}{k^2+\gamma^2}=1$$
> The [[6.9 - Particle Flux#^by8z3w|Probability Current]] for $x>0$ vanishes since $Te^{-\gamma x}$ is a real exponential up to a global phase, so $\psi^*\psi'-\psi\psi^{*'}=0$. There is nonzero probability density in the classically forbidden region, but no net flux. The superposition for $x<0$ forms a standing wave $\psi=2e^{-i\phi}\cos(kx+\phi)$ where $\tan\phi=\gamma/k$.

^step-below

## Potential Barrier

> [!def] Potential Barrier
> A symmetric rectangular barrier of height $V_0$ and half-width $a$:
> $$V(x)=\begin{cases}0 & |x|>a\\V_0 & |x|\leq a\end{cases}$$
> Solve the [[6.3 - Energy#^ecd6eb|Time-Invariant Schrödinger Equation]] in three regions and match $\psi$ and $\psi'$ at $x=\pm a$.

^barrier

> [!exm] Barrier: $E > V_0$ (Resonant Transmission)
> With $k=\sqrt{2mE}/\hbar$ outside and $k'=\sqrt{2m(E-V_0)}/\hbar$ inside, matching at $x=\pm a$ gives:
> $$|T|^2=\left[1+\left(\frac{k^2-k'^2}{2kk'}\right)^2\sin^2(2k'a)\right]^{-1}$$
> Perfect transmission $|T|^2=1$ occurs at resonance $2k'a=n\pi$, when the barrier width is a half-integer multiple of the de Broglie wavelength. As $E\to\infty$, $k'\to k$ and $|T|^2\to 1$.

^barrier-above

> [!thm] Quantum Tunnelling
> For $E<V_0$, define $\gamma=\sqrt{2m(V_0-E)}/\hbar$. Replacing $k'\to i\gamma$ using $\cos(ix)=\cosh x$ and $\sin(ix)=i\sinh x$, the transmission amplitude is:
> $$T=\frac{e^{-2ika}}{\cosh(2\gamma a)-i\,\dfrac{k^2-\gamma^2}{2k\gamma}\sinh(2\gamma a)}$$
> $$|T|^2=\left[\cosh^2(2\gamma a)+\left(\frac{k^2-\gamma^2}{2k\gamma}\right)^2\sinh^2(2\gamma a)\right]^{-1}$$
> This is nonzero for all finite $\gamma a$: the particle tunnels through the classically forbidden region. Writing $\epsilon=E/V_0$ and $\alpha=a\sqrt{2mV_0}/\hbar$:
> $$|T|^2=\left(1+\frac{\sinh^2\!\left(2\alpha\sqrt{1-\epsilon}\right)}{4\epsilon(1-\epsilon)}\right)^{-1}$$

^tunnelling

`\begin{proof}`@[[#^tunnelling]]
The general solution in the three regions is:
$$\psi(x)=\begin{cases}e^{ikx}+Re^{-ikx} & x<-a\\Ae^{\gamma x}+Be^{-\gamma x} & |x|\leq a\\Te^{ikx} & x>a\end{cases}$$
Matching $\psi$ and $\psi'$ at $x=-a$ and $x=a$ gives four equations in $R,A,B,T$. The tunnelling formula follows by substituting $k'\to i\gamma$ into the $E>V_0$ result for the same geometry, using the identities $\cos(i\theta)=\cosh\theta$ and $\sin(i\theta)=i\sinh\theta$.

The simplified form uses $k^2+\gamma^2=2mV_0/\hbar^2$ and $k\gamma=\sqrt{2mE\cdot 2m(V_0-E)}/\hbar^2$, giving:
$$\frac{(k^2+\gamma^2)^2}{(2k\gamma)^2}=\frac{1}{4\epsilon(1-\epsilon)}, \qquad 2\gamma a=2\alpha\sqrt{1-\epsilon}$$
The two forms are equivalent since $\cosh^2+\left(\frac{k^2-\gamma^2}{2k\gamma}\right)^2\sinh^2=1+\left(\frac{k^2+\gamma^2}{2k\gamma}\right)^2\sinh^2$.
`\end{proof}`
