# 6.8 - Scattering

> [!def] Reflection Coefficient
> Given an incident [[6.5 - Free Particles#^free-matter-wave|Free Matter Wave]] of amplitude $A$ scattering off a piecewise potential, the **reflection coefficient** $R$ is the ratio of the reflected amplitude $B$ to the incident amplitude.
>
> The algebra mirrors the classical [[4.4 - Plane Waves#^reflection-coefficient]], but the [[6.9 - Particle Flux#^probability-current|Probability Current]] $|\psi|^{2}$ carries a different meaning, since it is a probability density rather than an energy density.
> $$R\equiv \frac{B}{A}$$

^qm-reflection-coefficient

> [!def] Reflection Probability
> The **reflection probability** $\mathcal{P}(R)$ is the fraction of incident [[6.9 - Particle Flux#^probability-current|Probability Current]] $j=(\hbar k/m)|A|^{2}$ that returns to the incident region, given the [[#^qm-reflection-coefficient]] $R$.
>
> Since the reflected wave shares the incident wavenumber $k$, the velocity factors in the flux cancel:
> $$\mathcal{P}(R)\equiv\frac{j_{\text{refl}}}{j_{\text{inc}}}=|R|^{2}$$

^qm-reflection-probability

> [!def] Transmission Coefficient
> Given an incident [[6.5 - Free Particles#^free-matter-wave|Free Matter Wave]] of amplitude $A$ scattering off a piecewise potential, the **transmission coefficient** $T$ is the ratio of the transmitted amplitude $C$ to the incident amplitude.
>
> The algebra mirrors the classical [[4.4 - Plane Waves#^transmission-coefficient]], but encodes a probability of transmission rather than a fraction of transmitted energy.
>
> $$T\equiv \frac{C}{A}$$

^qm-transmission-coefficient

> [!def] Transmission Probability
> The **transmission probability** $\mathcal{P}(T)$ is the fraction of incident [[6.9 - Particle Flux#^probability-current|Probability Current]] $j=(\hbar k/m)|A|^{2}$ that continues into the far region, given the [[#^qm-transmission-coefficient]] $T$.
>
> The transmitted wave has a different group velocity $\hbar k_{\text{far}}/m$ from the incident, so the velocity ratio does not cancel and shows up as a $k_{\text{far}}/k_{\text{inc}}$ prefactor:
>
> $$\mathcal{P}(T)\equiv\frac{j_{\text{trans}}}{j_{\text{inc}}}=\frac{k_{\text{far}}}{k_{\text{inc}}}|T|^{2}, \qquad \mathcal{P}(R)+\mathcal{P}(T)=1$$
>
> The sum equals one by [[6.9 - Particle Flux#^continuity-equation|Conservation of Probability]], just as in the classical case where $\mathcal{P}(R)+\mathcal{P}(T)=1$ encodes energy conservation across the boundary.

^qm-transmission-probability

## Step Potential

> [!def] Step Potential
> A piecewise constant potential with a single discontinuity at $x=0$:
> $$V(x)=\begin{cases}0 & x<0\\V_0 & x\geq 0\end{cases}$$
> Solve the [[6.3 - Energy#^time-invariant-schrdinger-equation|Time-Invariant Schrödinger Equation]] in each region,
>
> $$-\frac{\hbar^{2}}{2m}\frac{d^{2}\psi}{dx^{2}}+V(x)\,\psi(x)=E\,\psi(x)$$
>
> The boundary conditions are:
> 1. $$\psi(x>0)=Te^{ik'x}\text{ only (no leftward wave for }x>0\text{)}$$
> 2. $$\psi(0^{-})=\psi(0^{+})$$
> 3. $$\psi'(0^{-})=\psi'(0^{+})$$
> 
> The two energy regimes $E>V_{0}$, $E<V_{0}$ give the [[#^step-above|Unbound]] and [[#^step-potential-ev-below-0|Bound]] solutions.

^step-potential

### Unbound

> [!thm] Step Potential - Unbound State
> For a particle incident from the left with $E>V_0$, the [[#^qm-reflection-coefficient|Reflection]] and [[#^qm-transmission-coefficient|Transmission]] amplitudes are set by the wavenumber mismatch between the two regions, with $k=\sqrt{2mE}/\hbar$ outside and $k'=\sqrt{2m(E-V_0)}/\hbar$ on the step:
> $$R=\frac{k-k'}{k+k'},\qquad T=\frac{2k}{k+k'}$$
>
> The corresponding [[#^qm-reflection-probability|Reflection]] and [[#^qm-transmission-probability|Transmission Probabilities]] sum to one:
> $$|R|^2=\left(\frac{k-k'}{k+k'}\right)^2,\qquad \mathcal{P}(T)=\frac{k'}{k}|T|^2=\frac{4kk'}{(k+k')^2}$$

^step-above

`\begin{proof}`@[[#^step-above]]
For $E>V_{0}>0$, the [[6.3 - Energy#^time-invariant-schrdinger-equation|Time-Invariant Schrödinger Equation]] $-(\hbar^{2}/2m)\psi''=(E-V(x))\psi$ is a [[4.1 - Simple Harmonic Oscillation#^d9cde8|Simple Harmonic ODE]] in each region, with general solution

$$\psi(x)=\begin{cases}Ae^{ikx}+Be^{-ikx} & x<0\\Ce^{ik'x}+De^{-ik'x} & x\geq 0\end{cases}$$

which reduces to waves at the boundary using BC 1, $D=0$, and normalising by $A$:

$$\frac{\psi(x)}{A}=\begin{cases}e^{ikx}+Re^{-ikx} & x<0\\Te^{ik'x} & x\geq 0\end{cases}$$

The wavefunction and its derivative are continuous at $x=0$, so the solution decomposes into reflection and transmission. BCs 2 and 3 give the scalar relations

$$\psi(0):\ 1+R=T,\qquad \psi'(0):\ ik(1-R)=ik'T$$

a system in $(R,T)$ whose [[2.3 - RREF and Systems of Equations#^9a9cd2|RREF]] reads off both directly:

$$\begin{bmatrix}1 & -1\\k & k'\end{bmatrix}\!\begin{bmatrix}R\\T\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}-1\\k\end{bmatrix}\quad\xrightarrow{\text{RREF}}\quad\mathbf{I}\,\begin{bmatrix}R\\T\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\tfrac{k-k'}{k+k'}\\[0.4em]\tfrac{2k}{k+k'}\end{bmatrix}$$

the same result as the classical [[4.4 - Plane Waves#^reflection-coefficient|Plane Wave]] reflection and transmission. Computing the incoming and reflected [[6.9 - Particle Flux#^probability-current|Probability Current]] in $x<0$, the cross terms cancel pairwise:

$$S_{<}=\frac{\hbar}{2mi}\!\left[\psi^{*}\frac{\partial\psi}{\partial x}-\psi\frac{\partial\psi^{*}}{\partial x}\right]=\frac{\hbar k}{2m}\!\left[(1-|R|^{2}-Re^{-2ikx}+R^{*}e^{2ikx})+(1-|R|^{2}+Re^{-2ikx}-R^{*}e^{2ikx})\right]=\frac{\hbar k}{m}(1-|R|^{2})$$

The transmitted current uses $\psi=Te^{ik'x}$, and reduces to $|T|^{2}$ times the group velocity $v_{>}=\hbar k'/m$:

$$S_{>}=\frac{\hbar}{2mi}\!\left[(T^{*}e^{-ik'x})(ik')(Te^{ik'x})+(Te^{ik'x})(ik')(T^{*}e^{-ik'x})\right]=\frac{\hbar k'}{m}|T|^{2}=|T|^{2}v_{>}=S_{T}$$

Conservation of probability $S_{<}=S_{>}$ then reads $\hbar k(1-|R|^{2})/m=\hbar k'|T|^{2}/m$, i.e. $|R|^{2}+\mathcal{P}(T)=1$ with

$$|R|^{2}=\!\left(\frac{k-k'}{k+k'}\right)^{2},\qquad \mathcal{P}(T)=\frac{k'}{k}|T|^{2}=\frac{4kk'}{(k+k')^{2}}$$
`\end{proof}`

### Bound

> [!thm] Step Potential - Bound State
> For $E<V_0$, the particle is classically forbidden inside the step but penetrates a finite depth as an evanescent tail. Define $\gamma=\sqrt{2m(V_0-E)}/\hbar$; reflection is total, and the incident plus reflected waves form a [[4.6 - Standing Waves#^287dbf|Standing Wave]] with phase shift $\tan\phi=\gamma/k$:
> $$|R|^{2}=1,\qquad \psi(x<0)=2e^{-i\phi}\cos(kx+\phi)$$
>
> Probability density is nonzero in the forbidden region, but the [[6.9 - Particle Flux#^probability-current|Probability Current]] there vanishes — there is leakage but no flux.

^step-potential-ev-below-0

`\begin{proof}`@[[#^step-potential-ev-below-0]]
For a particle below the mirror potential $0<E<V_{0}$, the eigenvalue equation in $x>0$ becomes $\psi''=\gamma^{2}\psi$, a [[4.1.3 - Constant Coefficient ODEs#^382f29|Constant Coefficient ODE]] with real roots $\pm\gamma$ where $\gamma=\sqrt{2m(V_{0}-E)}/\hbar$. Equivalently, $k'=\sqrt{2m(E-V_{0})}/\hbar=i\gamma$, so the [[#^step-above|Unbound State]] solution carries over under $k'\to i\gamma$. Applying BC 1, the rightward-decaying branch survives:

$$\psi(x)=\begin{cases}e^{ikx}+Re^{-ikx} & x<0\\Te^{-\gamma x} & x\geq 0\end{cases}$$

Solving the system in $(R,T)$ via [[2.3 - RREF and Systems of Equations#^9a9cd2|RREF]]:

$$\begin{bmatrix}1 & -1\\ik & \gamma\end{bmatrix}\!\begin{bmatrix}R\\T\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}-1\\ik\end{bmatrix}\quad\xrightarrow{\text{RREF}}\quad\mathbf{I}\,\begin{bmatrix}R\\T\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\tfrac{ik-\gamma}{ik+\gamma}\\[0.4em]\tfrac{2ik}{ik+\gamma}\end{bmatrix}$$

Multiplying numerator and denominator of $R$ by $-i$ gives the standard form $R=(k-i\gamma)/(k+i\gamma)$, and reflection is total:

$$|R|^{2}=\frac{(k-i\gamma)(k+i\gamma)}{(k+i\gamma)(k-i\gamma)}=\frac{k^{2}+\gamma^{2}}{k^{2}+\gamma^{2}}=1$$

The current in $x>0$ uses $\psi=Te^{-\gamma x}$, real up to a global phase, so $\psi^{*}\psi'-\psi\psi^{*\prime}=0$ and $S_{>}=0$; consistency with $S_{<}=(\hbar k/m)(1-|R|^{2})=0$ confirms there is no net flux despite nonzero probability density inside the forbidden region. Writing $k+i\gamma=\sqrt{k^{2}+\gamma^{2}}\,e^{i\phi}$ with $\tan\phi=\gamma/k$, the reflection coefficient is the pure phase $R=e^{-2i\phi}$, and for $x<0$,

$$e^{ikx}+Re^{-ikx}=e^{-i\phi}\!\left(e^{i(kx+\phi)}+e^{-i(kx+\phi)}\right)=2e^{-i\phi}\cos(kx+\phi),\qquad |\psi|^{2}=4\cos^{2}(kx+\phi)$$

a [[4.6 - Standing Waves#^287dbf|Standing Wave]] fixed in space, with $\phi$ playing the role of the boundary offset from the [[4.6 - Standing Waves#^2135a5|Open Standing Wave]] geometry. The transmission amplitude factors similarly,

$$T=\frac{2k}{k+i\gamma}=\frac{2k}{\sqrt{k^{2}+\gamma^{2}}}\,e^{-i\phi}=2\cos\phi\,e^{-i\phi}$$

so the evanescent tail in $x>0$ has

$$\psi(x)=2\cos\phi\,e^{-i\phi}\,e^{-\gamma x},\qquad |\psi|^{2}=4\cos^{2}\phi\,e^{-2\gamma x}$$
`\end{proof}`

## Potential Barrier

> [!def] Potential Barrier
> A symmetric rectangular barrier of height $V_0$ and half-width $a$. The geometry is the sign-flipped [[6.5 - Potential Wells#^finite-square-well|Finite Square Well]]: same three-region split at $x=\pm a$, but the well's $-V_{0}$ becomes $+V_{0}$, so oscillatory and decaying regions swap roles compared to the well's bound states.
> $$V(x)=\begin{cases}0 & |x|>a\\V_0 & |x|\leq a\end{cases}$$
> Solve the [[6.3 - Energy#^time-invariant-schrdinger-equation|Time-Invariant Schrödinger Equation]] in three regions:
> $$-\frac{\hbar^{2}}{2m}\frac{d^{2}\psi}{dx^{2}}+V(x)\,\psi(x)=E\,\psi(x)$$
>
> The boundary conditions are:
> 1. $$\psi(x>a)=Te^{ikx}\text{ only (no leftward wave for }x>a\text{)}$$
> 2. $$\psi(\pm a^{-})=\psi(\pm a^{+})$$
> 3. $$\psi'(\pm a^{-})=\psi'(\pm a^{+})$$
> 
> The two energy regimes $E>V_{0}$, $E<V_{0}$ give the [[#^resonant-transmission|Unbound]] and [[#^tunnelling|Bound]] solutions.

^potential-barrier

### Unbound

> [!thm] Potential Barrier - Unbound State
> For a [[#^potential-barrier]] with $E>V_{0}$, the [[#^qm-transmission-probability|Transmission Probability]] depends on the wavenumber mismatch and the phase $2k'a$ accumulated across the barrier, with $k=\sqrt{2mE}/\hbar$ outside and $k'=\sqrt{2m(E-V_0)}/\hbar$ inside:
> $$|T|^2=\left[1+\left(\frac{k^2-k'^2}{2kk'}\right)^2\sin^2(2k'a)\right]^{-1}$$
>
> Perfect transmission $|T|^2=1$ occurs at resonance $2k'a=n\pi$, when the barrier width is a half-integer multiple of the de Broglie wavelength inside, the same quantization that fixes [[4.6 - Standing Waves#^287dbf|Closed Standing Waves]]. As $E\to\infty$, $k'\to k$ and $|T|^2\to 1$.

^resonant-transmission

`\begin{proof}`@[[#^resonant-transmission]]
Applying BC 1, the general solution in the three regions is

$$\psi(x)=\begin{cases}e^{ikx}+Re^{-ikx} & x<-a\\Ce^{ik'x}+De^{-ik'x} & |x|\leq a\\Te^{ikx} & x>a\end{cases}$$

Applying BCs 2 and 3 at $x=\pm a$ gives a four-equation [[2.3 - RREF and Systems of Equations#^b09447|Linear System]] in $(R,C,D,T)$:

$$\begin{align}
\psi(-a):&\quad e^{-ika}+Re^{ika}=Ce^{-ik'a}+De^{ik'a} \\[0.5em]
\psi'(-a):&\quad k\big(e^{-ika}-Re^{ika}\big)=k'\big(Ce^{-ik'a}-De^{ik'a}\big) \\[0.5em]
\psi(+a):&\quad Ce^{ik'a}+De^{-ik'a}=Te^{ika} \\[0.5em]
\psi'(+a):&\quad k'\big(Ce^{ik'a}-De^{-ik'a}\big)=kTe^{ika}
\end{align}$$

In matrix form $A\,\mathbf{x}=\mathbf{b}$ with unknowns $\mathbf{x}=(R,C,D,T)^{T}$ and system determinant $\Delta=\det A=(k+k')^{2}e^{-2ik'a}-(k-k')^{2}e^{2ik'a}$, the [[2.3 - RREF and Systems of Equations#^9a9cd2]] gives the solution:

$$\begin{align}
\begin{bmatrix}
e^{ika} & -e^{-ik'a} & -e^{ik'a} & 0 \\
-ke^{ika} & -k'e^{-ik'a} & k'e^{ik'a} & 0 \\
0 & e^{ik'a} & e^{-ik'a} & -e^{ika} \\
0 & k'e^{ik'a} & -k'e^{-ik'a} & -ke^{ika}
\end{bmatrix}\!\begin{bmatrix}R\\C\\D\\T\end{bmatrix}&=\begin{bmatrix}-e^{-ika}\\-ke^{-ika}\\0\\0\end{bmatrix} \\[0.7em]
\xrightarrow{\text{RREF}}\quad\mathbf{I}\,\begin{bmatrix}R\\C\\D\\T\end{bmatrix}&=\begin{bmatrix}
\dfrac{-2i(k^{2}-k'^{2})\sin(2k'a)\,e^{-2ika}}{\Delta} \\[0.7em]
\dfrac{2k(k+k')\,e^{-i(k+k')a}}{\Delta} \\[0.7em]
\dfrac{2k(k'-k)\,e^{-i(k-k')a}}{\Delta} \\[0.7em]
\dfrac{4kk'\,e^{-2ika}}{\Delta}
\end{bmatrix}
\end{align}$$

Reading off $T$ and dividing numerator and denominator by $4kk'$:

$$T=\frac{4kk'\,e^{-2ika}}{\Delta}=\frac{e^{-2ika}}{\cos(2k'a)-i\,\dfrac{k^{2}+k'^{2}}{2kk'}\sin(2k'a)}$$

Inverting and multiplying through by $e^{2ika}$ gives a single relation between the incident amplitude $1$ and $T$:

$$\frac{1}{T}=e^{2ika}\!\left(\cos(2k'a)-i\,\frac{k^{2}+k'^{2}}{2kk'}\sin(2k'a)\right)$$

Taking the norm square collapses the phase factor. Using $\cos^{2}+\sin^{2}=1$ and $(k^{2}+k'^{2})^{2}-(2kk')^{2}=(k^{2}-k'^{2})^{2}$:

$$\frac{1}{|T|^{2}}=\cos^{2}(2k'a)+\left(\frac{k^{2}+k'^{2}}{2kk'}\right)^{2}\sin^{2}(2k'a)=1+\left(\frac{k^{2}-k'^{2}}{2kk'}\right)^{2}\sin^{2}(2k'a)$$

Using dimensionless variables $\epsilon=E/V_{0}$ and $\alpha=a\sqrt{2mV_{0}}/\hbar$, the prefactor $((k^{2}-k'^{2})/(2kk'))^{2}=1/(4\epsilon(\epsilon-1))$ and $2k'a=2\alpha\sqrt{\epsilon-1}$, giving the simplified form

$$|T|^{2}=\!\left(1+\frac{\sin^{2}\!\left(2\alpha\sqrt{\epsilon-1}\right)}{4\epsilon(\epsilon-1)}\right)^{-1}$$

When $E\to\infty$, $k'\to k$ and the bracket vanishes, the particle has enough energy to overcome any barrier, so $|T|^{2}\to 1$. When $2k'a=n\pi$, equivalently $2a=n\lambda$ for the de Broglie wavelength $\lambda=2\pi/k'$ inside, the [[3.7.1 - Sinusoidals#^cef9d0|Sine]] kills the bracket and $|T|^{2}=1$, perfect transmission at the same quantization that fixes [[4.6 - Standing Waves#^287dbf|Closed Standing Waves]].
`\end{proof}`

### Bound

> [!thm] Potential Barrier - Bound State
> For $E<V_0$, define $\gamma=\sqrt{2m(V_0-E)}/\hbar$. The [[#^qm-transmission-probability|Transmission Probability]] is nonzero for any finite barrier, so the particle **tunnels** through the classically forbidden region. The [[3.7.1 - Sinusoidals#^cosh|Hyperbolic Cosine]] and [[3.7.1 - Sinusoidals#^sinh|Sine]] arise from the real-exponential interior solution:
> $$|T|^2=\left[\cosh^2(2\gamma a)+\left(\frac{k^2-\gamma^2}{2k\gamma}\right)^2\sinh^2(2\gamma a)\right]^{-1}$$
>
> In the dimensionless variables $\epsilon=E/V_0$ and $\alpha=a\sqrt{2mV_0}/\hbar$, this collapses to a single expression in $\epsilon$ and $\alpha$:
> $$|T|^2=\left(1+\frac{\sinh^2\!\left(2\alpha\sqrt{1-\epsilon}\right)}{4\epsilon(1-\epsilon)}\right)^{-1}$$

^tunnelling

`\begin{proof}`@[[#^tunnelling]]
For $E<V_{0}$, the inside region satisfies $\psi''=\gamma^{2}\psi$ with real exponential solutions, the same equation governing the outside region of the [[6.5 - Potential Wells#^finite-square-well|Finite Square Well]]. Applying BC 1, with oscillatory tails outside the barrier, the general solution is

$$\psi(x)=\begin{cases}e^{ikx}+Re^{-ikx} & x<-a\\Ce^{\gamma x}+De^{-\gamma x} & |x|\leq a\\Te^{ikx} & x>a\end{cases}$$

Applying BCs 2 and 3 at $x=\pm a$ gives a four-equation system in $(R,C,D,T)$:

$$\begin{align}
\psi(-a):&\quad e^{-ika}+Re^{ika}=Ce^{-\gamma a}+De^{\gamma a} \\[0.5em]
\psi'(-a):&\quad ik\big(e^{-ika}-Re^{ika}\big)=\gamma\big(Ce^{-\gamma a}-De^{\gamma a}\big) \\[0.5em]
\psi(+a):&\quad Ce^{\gamma a}+De^{-\gamma a}=Te^{ika} \\[0.5em]
\psi'(+a):&\quad \gamma\big(Ce^{\gamma a}-De^{-\gamma a}\big)=ikTe^{ika}
\end{align}$$

This is the [[#^resonant-transmission|Resonant Transmission]] system under $k'\to i\gamma$. In matrix form $A\,\mathbf{x}=\mathbf{b}$ with unknowns $\mathbf{x}=(R,C,D,T)^{T}$ and system determinant $\Delta_{\gamma}=\det A=(k+i\gamma)^{2}e^{2\gamma a}-(k-i\gamma)^{2}e^{-2\gamma a}$ setting the common denominator, [[2.3 - RREF and Systems of Equations#^9a9cd2|Row-Reducing]]:

$$\begin{align}
\begin{bmatrix}
e^{ika} & -e^{-\gamma a} & -e^{\gamma a} & 0 \\
-ike^{ika} & -\gamma e^{-\gamma a} & \gamma e^{\gamma a} & 0 \\
0 & e^{\gamma a} & e^{-\gamma a} & -e^{ika} \\
0 & \gamma e^{\gamma a} & -\gamma e^{-\gamma a} & -ike^{ika}
\end{bmatrix}\!\begin{bmatrix}R\\C\\D\\T\end{bmatrix}&=\begin{bmatrix}-e^{-ika}\\-ike^{-ika}\\0\\0\end{bmatrix} \\[0.7em]
\xrightarrow{\text{RREF}}\quad\mathbf{I}\,\begin{bmatrix}R\\C\\D\\T\end{bmatrix}&=\begin{bmatrix}
\dfrac{2(k^{2}+\gamma^{2})\sinh(2\gamma a)\,e^{-2ika}}{\Delta_{\gamma}} \\[0.7em]
\dfrac{2k(k+i\gamma)\,e^{(\gamma-ik)a}}{\Delta_{\gamma}} \\[0.7em]
\dfrac{-2k(k-i\gamma)\,e^{-(\gamma+ik)a}}{\Delta_{\gamma}} \\[0.7em]
\dfrac{4ik\gamma\,e^{-2ika}}{\Delta_{\gamma}}
\end{bmatrix}
\end{align}$$

Tunnelling happens when $E<V_{0}$. Applying the [[3.7.1 - Sinusoidals#^cosh|Hyperbolic]] substitutions $\cos(i\theta)=\cosh\theta$, $\sin(i\theta)=i\sinh\theta$ to the $1/T$ form from [[#^resonant-transmission|Resonant Transmission]], with $(k^{2}+k'^{2})/(2kk')\to(k^{2}-\gamma^{2})/(2k\gamma)$ since $k'^{2}=-\gamma^{2}$:

$$\frac{1}{T}=e^{2ika}\!\left(\cosh(2\gamma a)-i\,\frac{k^{2}-\gamma^{2}}{2k\gamma}\sinh(2\gamma a)\right)$$

Taking the norm squared, the bracket is strictly positive, so transmission persists despite the classically forbidden region — the particle **tunnels**:

$$\frac{1}{|T|^{2}}=\cosh^{2}(2\gamma a)+\left(\frac{k^{2}-\gamma^{2}}{2k\gamma}\right)^{2}\sinh^{2}(2\gamma a)\neq 0$$

To reach the $\epsilon,\alpha$ form, use $k^{2}+\gamma^{2}=2mV_{0}/\hbar^{2}$ and $k\gamma=\sqrt{2mE\cdot 2m(V_{0}-E)}/\hbar^{2}$, which give

$$\left(\frac{k^{2}+\gamma^{2}}{2k\gamma}\right)^{2}=\frac{V_{0}^{2}}{4E(V_{0}-E)}=\frac{1}{4\epsilon(1-\epsilon)},\qquad 2\gamma a=2\alpha\sqrt{1-\epsilon}$$

The [[3.7.1 - Sinusoidals#^hyperbolic-pythagorean|Hyperbolic Pythagorean Identity]] $\cosh^{2}-\sinh^{2}=1$ rewrites the bracket using the *plus* combination, which plugs cleanly into $\epsilon,\alpha$:
$$\cosh^{2}\theta+\left(\frac{k^{2}-\gamma^{2}}{2k\gamma}\right)^{2}\sinh^{2}\theta=1+\left(\frac{k^{2}+\gamma^{2}}{2k\gamma}\right)^{2}\sinh^{2}\theta$$

$$|T|^{2}=\!\left(1+\frac{\sinh^{2}\!\left(2\alpha\sqrt{1-\epsilon}\right)}{4\epsilon(1-\epsilon)}\right)^{-1}$$
`\end{proof}`
